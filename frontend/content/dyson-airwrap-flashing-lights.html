Quick answer

Check your Airwrap model and the light you saw before following a troubleshooting step. On the Airwrap i.d., Dyson’s manual pairs the light by the filter with a clean-the-filter instruction. If a red light continues after the approved filter care, use Dyson support.

The Airwrap i.d. manual shows one indicator at the bottom of the handle, just above the filter cage. Its key gives three meanings: flashing blue means ready to connect to the MyDyson app, constant blue means connected, and a separate indicator means clean the filter. It adds one firm rule: if the red LED lights and the filter has already been cleaned, contact the Dyson Helpline.

The manual’s key draws the indicators as flashing blue, red and white symbols beside a label marked “FILTRE”. If you are unsure which colour you saw, take any flashing light by the filter as a prompt to clean it first, and any red that returns afterwards as a reason to contact Dyson.

Clean the filter the approved way

Unplug the Airwrap and let it cool before touching the filter cage. The i.d. box includes a filter cleaning brush, and the manual has a regular filter maintenance section with the removal steps. Our Dyson Airwrap filter cleaning guide walks through that procedure. Do not rinse parts that the manual does not say can get wet, and refit the cage as directed before switching on again.

Worked example: the light by the filter flashes halfway through drying. You unplug, let the body cool, remove the cage, brush away lint and product residue, refit it and try again. If the light has stopped, carry on. If a red light appears again with a clean filter, you have reached the manual’s contact-Dyson point.

If you own a different Airwrap

Older Airwrap models and the Co-anda2x have their own manuals and support pages. Do not assume the i.d. key applies to them. Find the model on the handle or the box, then open Dyson’s Airwrap support page for that model. If you need help locating the right document, use the Hair Tool Manual Finder.

Dyson’s Supersonic dryers use a different light layout, covered on our Supersonic flashing lights page. For other tools that stop or flash, start with the styling tool troubleshooting guide.

When to stop using it

Stop and contact Dyson if you see damage to the cord or body, notice a burning smell, or the red light continues after the filter is clean. Do not open the appliance or keep restarting it to clear the light.
