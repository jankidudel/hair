T3 offers the US SinglePass Curl X with a 1-inch or 1¼-inch barrel, among its size choices. The quarter-inch difference is 6.35 mm. T3 also gives a separate iron weight for each size and specifies that the cord is excluded. Those are the two published differences between these sizes on the one T3 listing that sells them both. The same page describes an extra-long 6.5-inch barrel for the product, without saying which size that figure belongs to, so this guide keeps length out of the size comparison.

Compare the two barrel sizes

The figures come from the T3 US SinglePass Curl X listing. Millimetres are conversions at 25.4 mm per inch.

What T3 includes in the weight

T3 lists “1″ Iron Weight: 10.6oz (cord not included)” and “1.25″ Iron Weight: 13.2oz (cord not included)”. The 1¼-inch iron is listed 2.6 oz heavier. That is the iron alone, so the weight in your hand while styling will also depend on the cord, which neither figure includes. The 1.5″ size on the same chooser is listed at 15.1 oz on the same basis.

The listing’s “Extra-Long Barrel (6.5″)” appears in the product’s features rather than against one size. Don’t attach 6.5 inches to either barrel in a comparison unless T3 states it for that size.

Check the size on your current tool

With the tool off and cool, measure across the heated barrel. Write the figure in millimetres and compare it with 25.4 mm and 31.75 mm. For what diameter, usable length and taper each answer, see the curling iron barrel size guide.

Worked case: a 25 mm barrel you already use. Your current curling iron measures 25 mm across. The 1-inch Curl X converts to 25.4 mm, so it is the nearer size. The 1¼-inch is 6.75 mm wider than your 25 mm barrel.

Worked case: choosing on the right page. You find a 1¼-inch Curl X on a retailer page that gives a different weight. Check the size you picked on T3’s own size chooser; that is where the per-size weight is stated. If you are also weighing a ghd curler, its sizes are metric and published on a separate ghd page. Compare diameters in the same unit, and read T3 SinglePass Curl X vs ghd Soft Curl for that cross-brand comparison.

If you are buying either size second-hand, ask the seller which size it is and for a photo of any marking on the iron. The two irons share a name and a look, so the listing title alone may not tell you whether it is the 1-inch or the 1¼-inch. The weights T3 lists can help you check a seller’s description, but only if the seller weighs the iron without the cord, as T3 does.

A quarter inch can look small in a listing. Drawn at the same scale, 25.4 mm and 31.75 mm are easier to judge against the barrel you already have.
