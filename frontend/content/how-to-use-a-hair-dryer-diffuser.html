A diffuser spreads dryer airflow across a section instead of concentrating it in one direction. Start with hair that is comfortably blotted, choose moderate settings and work in sections you can hold without rushing.

Set up the dryer

Fit the diffuser securely, then begin with moderate heat and airflow. Settings are personal: the useful choice is one that feels controllable while you work around the roots and ends.

Dry in manageable sections

Bring a section gently toward the bowl of the diffuser and pause rather than constantly moving the attachment through it. Move to the next section once it feels mostly dry, then revisit areas that need more time.

Adjust for your preferred finish

Use a little less airflow when you want a calmer finish, or change the angle when you need to reach the roots. The aim is an ordinary styling routine, not a fixed technique to follow perfectly.
