The black ghd Helios lists a contoured nozzle. ghd also lists an 88 mm Wide Styling Nozzle and a Comb Nozzle for Helios, each on its own accessory page. So there are three nozzle-type pieces to think about. The first comes with the dryer. The other two are sold separately and you choose them for a particular job. This guide compares what ghd UK publishes for each, flags the measurements ghd leaves out, and shows how to check a replacement before you order it.

The nozzle supplied with black Helios

The ghd UK black Helios listing (p-497) names a contoured nozzle with the dryer. It doesn’t publish the nozzle’s width or a separate spare-part code for it. Other colours and gift sets need their own listing before you assume the same piece.

Wide and comb attachments

The Wide Styling Nozzle and the Comb Nozzle each list the Helios on their own page. The only published measurement is the wide nozzle’s 88 mm. That is the width of the nozzle, not a promise about how much hair it covers in a pass. To see every concentrator-type option with its fit evidence, open the Hair Dryer Attachment Finder for Helios concentrators.

Worked case: choosing between wide and comb. You already have the contoured nozzle and want a second attachment. Both are listed for Helios, so fit isn’t what separates them. Decide by the design ghd describes, a wide 88 mm outlet or a comb nozzle, and the way you section and dry your hair.

Check the exact part if you need a replacement

A missing original nozzle is the harder case, because ghd doesn’t give the contoured nozzle its own accessory code in the listing we hold. Note your dryer’s colour and the name on the label, then ask ghd for the replacement that matches your Helios. A nozzle from another ghd dryer isn’t evidence of fit, and an Air accessory page that doesn’t name Helios leaves the pairing unconfirmed.

Worked case: replacing a lost original nozzle. Your black Helios has lost its contoured nozzle. The wide and comb nozzles are listed for Helios, so either can be used, but neither is the original piece. For how replacement nozzles are matched on other dryers, read hair dryer replacement nozzle compatibility.

Buying a used Helios bundle? Ask for a photo that shows each nozzle separately next to the dryer, and the label on the handle. A listing that says “all attachments” doesn’t tell you whether the wide or comb nozzle is included, because ghd lists those on separate accessory pages rather than with the black Helios. Mark anything you can’t see as unconfirmed and ask the seller before you pay. A photo of the pieces laid out together is more useful than a stock image of a new box.

If what you actually want is a diffuser, ghd’s p-281 diffuser page gives the Helios its own adapter-ring instruction. Read ghd Air and Helios diffuser compatibility before ordering.
