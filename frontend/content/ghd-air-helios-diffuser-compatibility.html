The ghd Professional Hair Dryer Diffuser fits the UK ghd Air and Helios. Check your dryer’s model before fitting the ring, because ghd gives a separate instruction for the Air 1.0. The diffuser is sold on its own page, listed by ghd as p-281, and that page is a fit statement rather than a list of what came with any dryer. This guide sets out what ghd says for each body, where the adapter ring comes in, and what to check before you order a diffuser or fit one you already have.

Which ghd dryers fit p-281?

The ghd p-281 product page says to use the diffuser with the Air and tells Helios owners to add the adapter ring. A footnote on the same page, and ghd’s support answer, say the Air 1.0 needs the ring. The same support answer rules out the Flight+. The rows below keep each statement with its source.

When to use the adapter ring

ghd gives two ring instructions. The p-281 product feature names the Helios. The footnote and support answer name the Air 1.0. ghd does not, in the sources we hold, say whether the ring is supplied with the diffuser, so check the p-281 listing before you order. Don’t assume a loose, unlabelled ring from a second-hand bundle is the one ghd means for your body. If you can’t match it to the p-281 listing, ask ghd.

Worked case: Helios owner, no ring pictured. You own a black Helios and see a p-281 diffuser for sale with no ring in the photos. The product feature tells Helios owners to fit the ring, so ask the seller whether it is included and check the p-281 listing for what ghd supplies.

Check your dryer before ordering

Look at the rating label and the paperwork for the exact name. “Air” on its own doesn’t tell you whether ghd’s Air 1.0 instruction applies. If you are still choosing between the two dryers, the ghd Air vs Helios comparison sets out their listed controls and nozzles.

Worked case: Air owner, revision unclear. Your label says ghd Air but you can’t tell whether it is the Air 1.0. ghd says the diffuser fits the Air, and that the Air 1.0 needs the ring, so the honest result is “fits, check your Air version for the ring”. Contact ghd with the label details. For how diffusers connect on other brands, see hair dryer diffuser compatibility.

A part that fits is still not a part your box contained. A dryer can be compatible with p-281 and sold without it. For the Helios nozzle options, compare the standard, wide and comb nozzles.
