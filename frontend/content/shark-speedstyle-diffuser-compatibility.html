Check the model code before ordering a Shark SpeedStyle diffuser. The UK and US listings use similar diffuser names, while Shark’s compatible-model lists identify the exact codes. SharkNinja sells at least three diffusers for this family: one UK part for the SpeedStyle Pro Flex and two US parts, one for the original SpeedStyle HD300 series and one for the Pro Flex. Each part page names the bodies it fits, and each list applies only in the market where it is sold. This guide matches the codes and shows what one UK box lists.

Find the part code for your market

The codes below come from SharkNinja’s UK Pro Flex Diffuser, US SpeedStyle DefrizzFast Diffuser and US Pro Flex DefrizzFast Diffuser pages, and the HD542UK Mocha Silver listing.

Models and diffuser codes by market. Check your own model in the Hair Dryer Attachment Finder with HD542UK.

The UK page lists HD500EU, HD500UK, HD542EU and HD542UK. The US XSKHD3DA page lists HD300, HD301, HD331, HD332, HD341HRG, HD341HWH, HD352 and HD331REF, and says it is “compatible only with Shark SpeedStyle (HD300 Series)”. The US XSKHD5DAMS page lists HD500, HD542, HD542C and HD541HBR.

Which kits list a diffuser?

The HD542UK Mocha Silver box lists the main unit, a Turbo Concentrator, a QuickSmooth Brush, a DefrizzFast Diffuser, a FrizzFighter Finishing Tool and an instruction booklet. That shows a diffuser in that kit. It doesn’t tell you the boxed diffuser’s spare code, and other colours need their own box list. For how the Pro and Pro Flex kits differ, see Shark SpeedStyle Pro vs Pro Flex.

Worked case: UK HD542UK, unknown spare code. Your HD542UK diffuser is missing and you want a replacement. Shark UK’s XSKHD5DMSEU page names HD542UK, so that part is listed for your body, even though the box only calls its piece a DefrizzFast Diffuser.

Check a used or replacement part

Similar names are where mistakes happen. “DefrizzFast Diffuser” appears on two US parts with different lists, and FlexStyle diffusers are another family again; see Shark FlexStyle diffuser compatibility.

Worked case: US HD331 offered a Pro Flex part. A seller offers XSKHD5DAMS for your US HD331. That part’s list names HD500, HD542, HD542C and HD541HBR, not HD331, so fit is unconfirmed; the part for HD331 is XSKHD3DA. The reverse is different: XSKHD3DA on a US HD542 is excluded, because Shark limits XSKHD3DA to the HD300 series.

A US list says nothing about a UK body, and a UK list says nothing about a US one. Keep the full code, suffix included, when you search or ask Shark.

For a second-hand diffuser, ask the seller for a photo of any code moulded or printed on the part, and for the model code on the dryer it came from. If the part has no readable code, its listing name alone can’t tell you which of the three diffusers it is. Send Shark support your body code, your market and the part code you intend to buy, and wait for a yes before ordering. The rating label on the dryer’s handle is the quickest place to find the body code.
