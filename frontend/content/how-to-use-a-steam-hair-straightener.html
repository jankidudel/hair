A steam-straightening session begins with the actual appliance instructions, not with adding water to an ordinary iron. Establish the model, permitted contents, hair preparation and fitted parts before switching on. This guide organises those checks around a named SteamPod 4 example without making every steam tool identical.

Quick answer

Match the manual to the appliance, prepare the hair as it requires, use only the documented reservoir contents and fit the instructed parts while the tool is off and cool. Follow its ready indication and styling direction, then switch off, unplug and complete its cooling routine. A steam system does not supply permission to style wet hair.

Identify the complete setup

The Rowenta support record identifies SteamPod 4 LP7400 / LP7400F1 and provides the LP74 instructions. Match the identity on your own tool before using that example. A different generation can have a different reservoir, comb or access method.

Set out the actual appliance, its approved parts and the instructions. Do not collect a bottle or attachment merely because it appears beside a related model in a shop photograph. Clear a suitable resting area and cord route before beginning the preparation steps.

Settle hair preparation and reservoir contents separately

For LP74, the manual specifies dried hair and commercially available demineralised water without additives. These are separate requirements. Do not replace the named water with perfume, conditioner or a styling formula, and do not put heat protectant in the reservoir.

For that model's filling step, release and lift the tank using its two lower buttons, open its tab and fill only to MAX with the specified water. Refit the front first, then click the back into place. Use the matching illustrations; this is not another model's tank-release method.

Any product application belongs on its own labelled path. Use heat-protectant application to organise coverage and readiness, while keeping both the product and appliance directions available. A product name does not override the tool's starting-state requirement.

Choosing between generations first? SteamPod 3 vs SteamPod 4 compares documented comb and design differences. Keep the preparation and reservoir sequence here attached to the exact tool you own. Historical SteamPod 3.0 brand guidance remains a generation-3 reference. The LP74 sequence here comes from its model-specific manual.

Check the comb and protective parts before heating

LP74 has model-specific comb choices and protective-cover instructions for straightening. Use the manual's illustrations for fitting and orientation; make changes only through the documented off/cool procedure. Do not assume that a part which looks like a storage guard must be removed for every styling task.

The UK SteamPod 4 FAQ also explains changing its removable comb when the tool is off and the plates have cooled. Keep that action separate from moving around a live, heated appliance. If a part does not fit normally, resolve the mismatch before starting.

Give each pass an organised working area

Park unused hair clear of the active section. Release an amount you can present without neighbouring strands entering the comb or hinge. Sectioning for styling helps organise the boundary; it does not prescribe a universal section size for this appliance.

For LP74, the manual's straightening direction places the steam outlets and comb toward the ends. Follow that named direction rather than turning the tool around to copy another iron's hand position. Use the documented ready state and technique; do not invent a shared pass speed, number of passes or highest setting.

Finish without turning a fault into another pass

If the tool leaks or operates abnormally, stop and follow its service instructions. Do not keep passing it over hair to see whether an apparent fault clears. A small visible steam plume, a finished appearance and an operating fault are different observations.

Complete shutdown, unplugging and cooling as directed. Use steam-straightener care for the separate maintenance decision. Routine care does not authorise opening the electrical body or repairing a reservoir seal.
