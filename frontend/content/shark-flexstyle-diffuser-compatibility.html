Quick answer

Find your FlexStyle body code, then check a diffuser listing for that exact code and market. An included diffuser, an optional diffuser and a part listing for a different model answer three different questions.

On 26 November 2026, the UK HD446UK box listed a Curl-defining Diffuser. The UK HD432PKUK and US HD430 boxes did not. The US Shark FlexStyle Curl-Defining Diffuser, XSKHD4DA, listed compatibility with HD400, HD435, HD440, HD430REF, HD435REF and HD432PK. The Canadian XSKHD4DA listing added HD430 and HD430C. So a diffuser in one box tells you nothing about another, and a part fits only the codes its listing names in the market where you buy it.

“Unconfirmed” means the evidence we read does not answer the question either way. It is not a no. It is a reason to ask Shark before paying.

Find your body code

The code is printed on the rating label of the main unit and on the box. Copy it exactly, including letters at the end. HD430, HD430REF and HD430C are different listings, and UK codes end in UK. A US HD430 owner should not rely on the Canadian list, and a UK owner should not rely on either North American list. If you cannot find the label or the paperwork, the Hair Tool Manual Finder can point you to the official manual or support page for your model.

Ask Shark when the list is silent

If your code is not on the part listing for your market, send Shark support a short question: “My body code is __, bought in __. Is part __ listed for that unit?” Keep the answer with your receipt. This is faster than ordering a part and returning it.

Worked example: you own a US HD430 and want to diffuse. Your box has no diffuser. The US XSKHD4DA listing names HD430REF but not HD430. Send the support question with HD430, US and XSKHD4DA, and wait for a yes before ordering.

A second example: you are choosing between UK boxes and diffusing matters to you. The HD446UK includes a diffuser in the box, while the HD432PKUK does not. The HD432PKUK listing also showed 120 volts in its specification block on the day we checked, so read the rating label or ask Shark to confirm UK compatibility before ordering that exact code.
