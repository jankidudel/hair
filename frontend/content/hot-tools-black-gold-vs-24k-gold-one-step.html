Hot Tools calls the Black Gold model on this page detachable and lists a separate 2.8-inch attachment and handle. It also gives the 24K Gold One-Step a 2.8-inch oval head label. The size labels alone do not tell you the full dimensions of either head. So the useful comparison between these two US listings is what each one actually says: whether the head comes off, what is included, and which settings are published. This guide sets those out word for word and leaves the head size as the label, because neither page gives a measured axis.

The details come from Hot Tools’ US Black Gold One-Step Detachable Blowout and 24K Gold One-Step Blowout listings. Neither page gives a model number.

What the 2.8-inch oval label measures

An oval head has a long axis and a short axis. A single 2.8-inch label could describe either, or something else, and neither listing says which. Don’t read it as the diameter of a round brush, and don’t assume two 2.8-inch labels describe heads of the same shape. For how shape and size answer different questions, read the hot air brush barrel size guide.

If you own a brush you like and want a direct comparison, measure it yourself. Measure across the widest point of the head, then across the narrowest, and enter the figures in the Hair Tool Size Visualiser. Neither Hot Tools head is offered as a preset there, because the label isn’t a measurement you can draw.

Worked case: comparing a round brush with an oval head. Your round brush measures 2 inches across the bristle tips. That figure is a diameter. The 2.8-inch oval label is not stated as one, so the two numbers can’t be subtracted. Ask Hot Tools what the label measures, or compare the heads in person.

Check the handle before buying a head

The Black Gold listing includes the attachment and the handle together. It doesn’t say which other heads that handle accepts. The 24K Gold listing doesn’t describe a removable head at all. So don’t buy a separate head on the assumption that it will fit either handle; check the exact head listing for the handle it names.

Worked case: you want a tool that’s explicitly detachable. Only the Black Gold listing uses the word detachable and lists the head and handle as separate pieces. If detachability matters to you, that’s the listing that states it. For how a Hot Tools One-Step compares with the Revlon One-Step Plus, see Hot Tools One-Step vs Revlon One-Step Plus.

When a listing from another seller describes either tool, check the name against the Hot Tools page. “One-Step” appears on both, so the colour and the words “Detachable” and “24K Gold” are what tell them apart in a listing title.

The 24K Gold listing also names an ALCI safety plug and a seven-year limited warranty, and the Black Gold listing a lifetime limited warranty. Check the current listing for warranty terms before you buy.
