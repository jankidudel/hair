Choose powered rotation if you want the head to turn while you guide the handle and are willing to manage direction buttons; choose a fixed head if you prefer to turn the whole tool yourself. Both have airflow. A body that rotates between dryer and styler modes is a third mechanism and does not prove that its brush head is motor driven.

Identify what actually rotates

The UK BaByliss Big Hair Deluxe AS970U listing identifies a 50 mm rotating brush and a separate 38 mm fixed bristle brush. This shows why an attachment-by-attachment reading matters. The US BaBylissPRO BABNT178 manual describes two rotation buttons, moving through a section and holding a chosen button to rotate at the end; it says rotation is not needed for the cool setting. Those exact instructions belong to BABNT178, not automatically to AS970U. The US Revlon Salon One-Step is a fixed oval-head hot-air brush that the user guides and turns by hand.

What a rotating routine asks of you

Read the exact appliance booklet for when rotation works, whether direction can be reversed and how to stop before unwinding a section. Keep sections manageable so hair does not collect at the joint or wrap beyond the intended area. The BABNT178 manual describes the user pressing and holding a direction button near the section end, then releasing it; that is a specific procedure, not a general instruction for every rotating brush. The AS970U product page documents two heat positions plus cool, but the current source here does not establish precisely how its rotation buttons behave at each setting. Leave that as an exact-manual check.

A fixed head avoids the direction-control step but makes the hand supply the turning motion. That can be a straightforward choice when you already have a routine with an oval hot-air brush. Do not infer faster drying, easier handling or a better finish from the presence of a motor. Powered movement changes the interaction, not the evidence for a universal result.

Check the attachment before the motor

A product may include several heads but rotate only one of them. The AS970U example lists a rotating 50 mm brush alongside a fixed 38 mm brush, paddle brush and straightening attachment. Do not call the entire bundle rotating without naming the head you would actually use. If the fixed smaller brush is the one that fits your sections, the powered feature may not affect your routine at all. Its listed dimensions are manufacturer labels, not evidence that every brush measures bristle span identically.
