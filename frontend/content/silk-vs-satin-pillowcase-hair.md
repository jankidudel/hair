<p>Silk and satin pillowcases are often discussed as if they are interchangeable. They are not. The useful decision is about feel, care, budget and whether you will actually keep using the pillowcase.</p>
<h2>Silk: natural fibre, more care</h2>
<p>Silk is a natural fibre and often feels smooth and lightweight. It can be more expensive and may require gentler washing. Check the fabric weight, construction and care label rather than assuming every silk product performs the same way.</p>
<h2>Satin: a weave, not a fibre</h2>
<p>Satin describes a smooth weave or finish. It can be made from synthetic fibres or silk. Synthetic satin options are often more affordable and easier to care for, though their feel and durability vary.</p>
<h2>Choose by your real habits</h2>
<p>If you want a lower-maintenance, budget-friendly option, satin may be the sensible starting point. If you value silk and are happy with its care requirements, a silk pillowcase may suit you. Buy a spare if you expect regular washing.</p>
<p>Consider either option a small routine preference, not a miracle upgrade. It may feel more comfortable than a rougher fabric, but it does not replace a good brush, sensible styling or a hair-care routine that suits you.</p>

<h2>The bottom line</h2>
<p>Silk is a fibre; satin is a weave. Pick the option whose feel, price and laundry requirements you are genuinely happy to live with.</p>
