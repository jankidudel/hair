A microfibre hair towel can make the first part of a drying routine feel less messy. Use it to blot excess water, then wrap only as tightly as feels comfortable.

Blot rather than rub

Press the towel gently along the lengths and ends. The goal is to remove excess water before your next styling step.

Make a comfortable wrap

Tip your head forward if that feels easy, place the towel over your hair and secure it according to its design without pulling.

Choose a routine

Remove it when you are ready to air dry or style. There is no need to leave it on for a prescribed period.
