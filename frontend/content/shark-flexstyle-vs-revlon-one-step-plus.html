Quick answer

If one oval head covers your routine, the Revlon One-Step Volumizer Plus (US) documents a detachable 2.4-inch oval head and low, medium, high and cool settings. If you would use several heads, the Shark FlexStyle HD430 in Stone (US) box lists two curlers, an oval brush, a paddle brush and a concentrator. Neither listing includes a diffuser.

Check what your current tool already covers

If you already own a dryer that handles rough drying, the question is which styling step you still need a tool for. Write down that step, then match it to a named head. The Revlon One-Step Volumizer Plus (US) is one oval head with named heat settings. The Shark FlexStyle HD430 in Stone (US) is a body with five listed attachments, so count only the heads you would actually use.

Work through two routines

If a working dryer already covers broad drying: Use the documented Revlon oval head as the simpler shortlist for the brush step. It gives you one detachable head and four named heat settings to compare against the brush you use now.

If you would use brush and airflow curling heads regularly: Map the listed HD430 attachments to those two jobs. Put the Oval Brush or Paddle Brush on the brush row and the two 1.25-inch Auto-Wrap Curlers on the curling row. If either row stays empty in your normal week, take that head out of the count before comparing the two setups again.
