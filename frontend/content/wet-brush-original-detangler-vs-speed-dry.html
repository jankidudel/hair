Quick answer

Choose Original Detangler for a standalone detangling brush. Choose Speed Dry if you want a vented brush the manufacturer describes as suitable for blow-drying. The two Frost Black models share a colour. Check the full model name before buying.

Decide whether one brush needs to do both jobs

If you detangle after washing and then dry without a brush, choose for grip and how easily the brush works through a normal section. If you already have a brush for detangling and want to guide hair under airflow, look at Speed Dry as the second tool. Hold an unplugged dryer and the brush together to see whether the handle and head give you enough control. The vents explain the design. Drying time will also depend on the dryer and how you section your hair.

Decide when detangling ends and drying begins

Think about how you prepare a usual section. If you use a hand-held brush to work through knots and then set it down, choose for the detangling step. Check whether the handle feels easy to hold and whether the head lets you work through a small section at a time. If you want to keep a brush under the section while holding a dryer in the other hand, give the documented dryer use and the grip more weight. The Frost Black Speed Dry listing expressly covers that job.

Consider a routine where the back tangles but you only style the ends under airflow. You might already have a detangling brush that works on the back. In that case, check the Speed Dry against the hand position you need for the ends first. If you mostly detangle and rarely use a hand-held brush with a dryer, you can choose the Original for the work you already do. The two brushes can make sense together when you use them on different steps.

Verify a listing that uses only the family name

Check the exact model title, bristle name and head in the US listing. For Speed Dry, the vents and the manufacturer’s description of blow-dryer use help identify the model. For Original Detangler, start with the exact Frost Black record and its stated detangling role. If a listing uses a manufacturer photo but gives vague instructions, open the maker’s version before deciding how you would use the brush.

A useful way to compare grip is to hold the brush and an unplugged dryer in the positions you normally use, without pulling through any knots. Note whether you can move the brush away before switching hands. That can matter more to your routine than the number of vents you see in a photograph.
