Quick answer

Choose the UK Supersonic Origin in Black/Nickel if you mostly use a concentrator and select the heat and airflow yourself. Consider Supersonic Nural in Ceramic Patina/Topaz if the included diffuser or its automatic controls solve a weekly problem. Many earlier Supersonic packages included different parts, so check the box you already have before comparing it with Origin.

Would you use those differences?

List the two attachments you use most and the setting you choose for each. If you use a concentrator for the whole blowout, a working Origin or older Supersonic may cover the job. If you diffuse regularly, confirm the Wave+Curl diffuser in the exact Nural package and check how its two configurations fit your routine.

Before replacing a dryer to get an attachment, check the exact part number. Physical compatibility and access to sensor-supported features are separate checks. Use the diffuser compatibility guide for fit and Supersonic Nural vs Supersonic r for the other Dyson design decision.

Compare a nozzle routine with a diffuser routine

For a nozzle-led blowout, list the heat and airflow choices you make most often. If you use the same settings through the lengths and rarely change attachments, the Origin gives you the concentrator on the named listing. If you sometimes set the dryer down to resection, read how Nural’s Pause Detect works with the attachments you would use and see whether it would change that part of the routine.

For a diffuser-led routine, start with the Wave+Curl diffuser and its two configurations. Check how you want the airflow to reach the section and whether you would regularly switch between configurations. Then decide which other included head, if any, you would use after diffusing. If you already own a working dryer and diffuser, use that combination as the benchmark. An upgrade is easier to justify when you can name an attachment or control you need frequently.

Verify a replacement box against what you already own

If your current Supersonic came with more than a concentrator, write down those head names before comparing the current Origin. The Origin box is a specific listing within the family. Check its included pieces against your existing setup and get Dyson’s compatibility confirmation for anything you hope to carry across. For Nural’s automatic features, check the support notes for the exact attachment and mode. A compatible physical fit answers only the first part of that question.

If you are buying for two people, have each person identify their main attachment and whether they would use an automatic mode. Compare the package against those jobs first and use price to decide after you know that the required parts are included.
