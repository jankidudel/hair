Start with the styling change you want. Bounce Curl describes Define EdgeLift as shaping medium-sized curl groups and Volume EdgeLift as separating smaller groups. Both are styling brushes, and both product directions begin with detangling first. The brush name does not guarantee a particular finish on your hair.

Quick answer

Choose Define EdgeLift if you want the maker's medium-sized curl grouping. Choose Volume EdgeLift if you want the maker's smaller, more separated grouping. If the high tension of both is the concern, the maker's Slick-Dense and Slick-Flex variants are the ones to look at instead. If your current brush already groups curls the way you like, neither is a necessary upgrade.

What the side edges change

The difference between the two brushes is along the side edges of the head. Define's listing describes a medium side-edge design; Volume's describes smaller side-edge spacing. As the brush passes through a section, those edges gather the hair into groups, so closer spacing is how the maker explains Volume's smaller groups. Why both need preparation first

Both product instruction blocks start with detangling, then styling product and sections, and only then the styling pass. The brand's broader comparison also lists wet detangling among Define's features, but a dense styling head is not the tool to drag through knots. Work through tangles with your usual detangler or a wide-tooth comb, apply product, divide the hair into sections you can control, then make the styling pass with Define or Volume.

For the difference between those two jobs, see curl-defining brush vs detangling brush. For section sizes and clipping, use how to section hair for styling.

Define and Volume versus the Slick variants

On the maker's brush comparison chart, Define and Volume are both in the high-tension category. Slick-Dense Define EdgeLift is listed as medium tension and Slick-Flex Define EdgeLift as low tension with spaced bristles; both Slick variants are described with medium-sized grouping. Some stockists describe Volume as medium tension; the maker's own chart does not, so this guide follows the chart. This comparison stops at that boundary: it does not compare every Slick model, colour or curl-pattern variation.

When to keep your current brush

If your existing styling brush already gives you the grouping you want, a different EdgeLift adds little. If you only need to detangle, neither brush is the right first purchase. These are styling tools for a defined step, not replacements for your detangler.

Care and what “vegan boar” means

The product pages describe the bristles as “vegan boar”. That is a brand description of a synthetic bristle made to resemble boar bristle, not animal boar hair. It does not by itself tell you the full material composition or any environmental benefit. For ordinary cleaning, remove trapped hair, wash the brush with a mild shampoo, rinse and let it air-dry. The product pages also mention disinfectant products; this guide sticks to the ordinary mild shampoo method and does not recommend mixing cleaning chemicals.
