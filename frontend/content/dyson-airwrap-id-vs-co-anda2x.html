Airwrap i.d. and Co-anda2x are different connected multi-styler generations. The most practical first question is whether the proposed body and attachments fit the routine and parts you already own. A shared Airwrap name does not establish a shared attachment interface or make a full replacement necessary.

Quick answer

Consider i.d. when its documented connected curling sequence and confirmed compatible parts meet the tasks you need. Consider Co-anda2x when its selected new attachments serve a specific additional job and you accept a separate attachment system. Keep a working Airwrap when the proposed kit mainly repeats existing roles.

Resolve attachment fit before counting pieces

Dyson's Co-anda2x compatibility FAQ says previous Airwrap and i.d. attachments do not fit Co-anda2x, and Co-anda2x parts do not fit those earlier bodies. Earlier Airwrap and i.d. attachments are described as cross-compatible. That changes the upgrade calculation: an existing collection of parts stays useful with an earlier or i.d. body, while a Co-anda2x purchase needs its own confirmed kit. Buying one 2x attachment never adds a Co-anda2x function to an i.d. body.

For the full body-by-attachment matrix, identification help and a quick lookup, use the Airwrap attachment compatibility guide. This comparison keeps the whole-machine buying decision.

Co-anda2x is not one fixed box

The attachments in a Co-anda2x box depend on the kit and colourway you select. The UK Ceramic apricot/Topaz example above lists AirSmooth2x. The current UK Straight+Wavy Plum/Copper listing, checked 19 November 2026, lists an Air Straightener 2x instead. These are different attachment names, not a rename to read across. The Plum/Copper page describes its Air Straightener 2x as working while hair dries, but it still carries older FAQ copy about AirSmooth2x as a dry-hair finishing stage, so this comparison does not publish one shared operating sequence for both. The Ceramic apricot comparison above remains the original selected example; the Plum/Copper offer is labelled separately here.

Give each proposed attachment a repeated job

List the tasks you actually use: preparation drying, brush-led shaping, airflow curling or a particular finishing stage. Place the confirmed included part beside each task and leave unsupported roles unverified. A large box is not a better fit when most of its parts duplicate the tools you already use.

The selected Ceramic apricot listing gives AirSmooth2x a specific finishing role, and other boxes list an Air Straightener 2x. Neither should be treated as the same thing as a similarly named older smoothing attachment. Read the relevant attachment's preparation and operating instructions before considering it a substitute for a different appliance.

Use multi-styler attachment selection to make the task list. This comparison owns the generation choice; the selection guide owns the wider fit-versus-box-content process.

For the curling task, compare airflow barrels with conventional wands, then inspect the airflow operating stages. That method's named i.d. example does not establish Co-anda2x controls or a common release cue. Resolve the exact generation before transferring a sequence from a demonstration.

Decide whether connected features matter to you

Inspect the documented setup and supported app functions for the exact generation. A personalised sequence is a control feature, not independent proof of consistent curls for every user. If your current routine already works through manual controls, app connectivity alone may not resolve a problem.

Consider accessory replacement and the current local package total together. This article does not reproduce a sale price or calculate value from a pressure claim. Any speed or pressure percentage on a Dyson page is the maker's own comparison against its own stated comparator, not a CombReport test, and this guide supplies no timed comparison of curling, drying or straightening performance.

Keep your system or change it?

Keep an existing earlier Airwrap or i.d. when it already performs the jobs you repeat. If one job is missing, check whether a compatible earlier-family part covers it before considering a new body.

Consider changing to Co-anda2x when a specifically documented Co-anda2x capability serves a job you will repeat, and you accept replacing both the body and the parts you rely on, because the older parts will not fit. This is editorial reasoning about what you would be paying to replace, not a guarantee of a better result. A model launch does not make a working routine incomplete.
