The current US One-Step Volumizer PLUS lists a detachable 2.4-inch oval head and four settings, including medium. Choose the PLUS if those exact head and control differences solve a handling or storage issue; choose the fixed-head version if you want fewer pieces. Keep a functioning brush when neither feature matters.

“Original” is often used casually for the first One-Step design, but the current manufacturer page says the Salon One-Step has new features. This article compares the two linked current US listings, not every historical RVDR version, colour, retailer bundle or UK appliance. What a different head changes

The detachable design can make packing the PLUS into a particular storage space easier, but it adds a joint and a step when attaching the brush. A 2.4-inch head changes the area that contacts a section compared with a larger or differently shaped head; the current Salon One-Step page does not publish a comparable exact head dimension in the material used here. Do not call one faster on that basis. Before buying, picture the sections you regularly style and whether you prefer one fixed tool or a handle and a removable head.

The PLUS page describes four settings. It does not establish a personalized temperature or guarantee a gentler result. The Salon One-Step page describes three heat/speed settings and says its head is not detachable. Both are hot-air brushes, so the barrel-size guide helps you think about section handling, while hot-air brush cleaning guidance covers ordinary care checks. Follow the exact manufacturer's cleaning and use instructions for the unit in hand.

Check the model label, not only the retail name

Think through the physical routine before viewing an additional setting as an automatic upgrade. If the brush lives assembled in a drawer and the existing fixed head reaches your usual sections, detachment may add little. If the storage space is narrow or you want the documented smaller PLUS head, check the release and reattachment steps in its instructions and confirm that the head you expect is actually included. The head must be seated as directed before use. A replaceable looking joint does not establish that heads from other One-Step generations will fit.

One-Step names span older and refreshed versions, other shapes, and regional electrical variants. If a retailer says “Original” but shows a detachable head, pause and compare its model code with the manufacturer listing. A UK Volumiser or Canadian Volumizer PLUS is not made electrically interchangeable with these US pages by a similar name. The official Canadian PLUS RVDR5298F page illustrates why a regional code should be recorded; it does not prove the code of the US item.

Compare the seller's photographs with the exact manufacturer page as a final check. Look for the fixed or detachable head, the named heat/speed settings, the market shown on the packaging and the instructions for the included parts. If any of these conflict, pause the comparison until the seller can identify its actual model. Neither article nor explorer treats a shared product-family name as evidence that electrical ratings, replacement heads or warranties transfer between markets.
