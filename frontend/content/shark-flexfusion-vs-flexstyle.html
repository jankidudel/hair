Start with the attachment you would use most often. For the Cosmic Blush Shark FlexFusion Straight HD652SUK (UK), Boots lists a wet-or-dry straightener and a separate brush among the advertised kit attachments. Shark’s own black FlexStyle HD446UK (UK) listing gives a different mix of brushes, directional curlers, a concentrator and an included diffuser. Both listings describe a diffuser for their respective bundles. If you already own a Shark styling body, compare which parts are supplied and check which parts the maker confirms will fit that body before you decide what you need.

Quick answer

Choose by the head you would reach for most. Boots lists a wet-or-dry straightener and a Fusion Brush for the FlexFusion Straight HD652SUK (UK). Shark lists no dedicated straightener in the FlexStyle HD446UK (UK) box, which has oval and paddle brushes and left and right curlers instead. Both listings include a concentrator, a diffuser and a storage case.

Check fit for the body you own

A part that appears in one box does not show that it fits another body. Shark UK sells a straightener attachment whose title names HD652SUK, but the compatibility field on that part page names other codes. Use the part title to identify the item you are asking about, and keep fit on any other body as an open question until Shark confirms it for your code.

If you own a FlexStyle and want to add a head rather than buy a whole kit, write down the code on the rating label and the exact part code. The same check applies to diffusers: Which diffuser fits my Shark FlexStyle? shows how Shark’s part listings name the bodies they fit.

Work through two routines

If you mainly diffuse: Circle the diffuser on the exact retailer or maker inventory. Both packages list a curl-defining diffuser, so that job alone leaves both of them under consideration. Move to the second head you would use each week to separate them. Shark FlexStyle models and kits compared shows which FlexStyle boxes include a diffuser if HD446UK is not the only one on your list.
