A hot-air brush for thick hair should make sectioning feel manageable. Look for a barrel you can control, settings you can adjust and a shape that works with your preferred finish.

Start with sections

Thick hair often benefits from dividing the routine into sections you can guide without tugging or rushing.

Compare barrel sizes

A larger barrel can support a broad, loose finish; a smaller barrel can give more direction around shorter layers.

Prioritise controls

Useful heat and airflow controls help you adapt the tool to different areas of the head.
