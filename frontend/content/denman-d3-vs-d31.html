Both the Denman D3 and D31 are seven-row brushes.

Quick answer

Choose by the arrangement you want to guide through the section. The UK D3 listing describes wet curl definition and precision blow-dry styling. Denman describes the D31 Freeflow layout as more widely spaced and staggered, with detangling and wet curl definition among its uses. If your existing D3 works with a smaller section, another seven-row brush may repeat a task you already cover. Inspect the exact variant and instructions before choosing a different layout.

Choose the job before counting rows

A brush used to prepare a section and one used to guide its final shape can occupy different places in a routine. Denman's descriptions give you a way to shortlist a layout, but they do not establish a universal detangling winner. Start with the part of your routine that catches or feels crowded. Keep preparation, styling tension and the finish you want as separate observations when comparing the two brushes.

Read spacing as a layout description

Denman's account of the D31 describes a more open arrangement. That is useful evidence about the design, while numerical spacing and comparable head dimensions remain unknown here. Avoid calculating a contact area from the seven-row label. If you can inspect both exact variants, look at the path a small section takes between the pins, the amount of hair that fills the working surface, and whether you can release it without pulling.

For the separate paddle-body decision, compare D3 vs D38. Its broader air-cushioned format answers a different question from the spacing of these two seven-row styling heads.

Try a smaller working section first

A crowded brush can reflect the amount of hair presented to it. Divide the working area, support the section and follow the model's permitted wet or dry use. If your current tool now moves through the task comfortably, that gives you a reason to keep it. A broader or more open layout is worth considering when it addresses a handling problem that remains after sectioning, rather than because seven rows sound too few.

Record the exact choice

Record the full D3 and D31 names, the described pin arrangement and the wet or dry task you intend. Leave numerical pin spacing blank until measured on those variants. If you are replacing a worn brush, compare its condition with the new product description before attributing every handling difference to the layout. Wear and a change of design can occur together.
