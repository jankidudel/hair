Quick answer

Consider i.d. when its documented connected curling sequence and confirmed compatible parts meet the tasks you need. Consider Co-anda2x when its selected new attachments serve a specific additional job and you accept a separate attachment system. Keep a working Airwrap when the proposed kit mainly repeats existing roles.

Resolve attachment fit before counting pieces

Dyson's Co-anda2x compatibility FAQ says that previous Airwrap and i.d. attachments do not fit Co-anda2x, and Co-anda2x parts do not fit those earlier devices. It separately identifies cross-compatibility between Airwrap and i.d. attachments. Confirm the particular part and permitted use rather than converting that family statement into an instruction to fit anything that looks similar.

This distinction changes an upgrade calculation. An existing collection of parts may remain useful with an appropriate earlier-generation body, while a Co-anda2x purchase needs its own confirmed kit. Do not assume buying one new attachment adds a Co-anda2x function to an i.d. body.

Give each proposed attachment a repeated job
