Check the Airwrap generation before buying another attachment. Dyson says earlier Airwrap and Airwrap i.d. attachments are cross-compatible, while Co-anda2x uses a separate attachment system. An older part does not become compatible with Co-anda2x because it is a Dyson accessory or does the same styling job.

Quick answer

If you own an earlier Airwrap or an Airwrap i.d., attachments from either of those two generations belong to one compatible family; confirm the exact part listing before you order. If you own a Co-anda2x, you need Co-anda2x (2x) parts: earlier and i.d. attachments do not fit, and 2x attachments do not fit the earlier bodies. No Dyson approval for third-party adapters was found, so this guide does not recommend one.

Identify your body and attachment

Find the product name on the original box, receipt, order confirmation or product registration, or in the model details on the appliance itself. Do the same for a loose attachment: the listing it was bought from, or its packaging, usually names the generation. In the Co-anda2x box listings checked for CombReport's comparisons, each attachment name carries a “2x” suffix, such as Fast dryer 2x or Wave+Curl diffuser 2x. Use that as a prompt to check the listing, not as proof on its own.

If the body or a second-hand part has no name you can trust, choose “I don't know” in the lookup and contact Dyson support before buying. A similar shape, colour or styling job is not a reliable way to identify a generation.

Why the direction matters

Owners usually ask one of two questions: will my old parts work on a new body, or will a new part work on my old body? For Co-anda2x the answer is no both ways. For earlier Airwrap and i.d. the family statement covers both directions, but a part designed around a later feature can still behave differently on an earlier body. Ask about the function you need, not only whether the part clicks on.

Check the exact part before ordering

This guide is deliberately not a part-code database. Dyson sells attachments individually and in kits, and its listings change. Before ordering, confirm that the exact part page names your body generation. For an Airwrap Origin or a particular conical barrel, use that part's own listing: this research did not verify those models individually, so they are not inferred from a family name.

Physical fit and connected features are separate

Airwrap i.d. and Co-anda2x both document connected features, such as a personalised curling sequence. An attachment that fits an earlier body physically does not, on that evidence alone, bring those features with it. If a connected feature is the reason you are buying, check that the exact body and part support it together.

Air Straightener 2x and AirSmooth2x are not the same name

The current UK Co-anda2x Straight+Wavy Plum/Copper listing includes an Air Straightener 2x, while other Co-anda2x boxes, including the UK Ceramic apricot example in our i.d. versus Co-anda2x comparison, list AirSmooth2x. The newer listing describes the Air Straightener 2x as working while hair dries, yet the same page still carries FAQ copy about AirSmooth2x as a dry-hair finishing stage. Regard them as two different attachment names in different selected packs. Use the exact attachment's own instructions rather than a sequence copied from a page that mixes both.

A new attachment pack or a new body?

If you own an earlier Airwrap or i.d. and want one more job, a compatible earlier-family part is the smaller change. A Co-anda2x part never adds a 2x function to an older body. Changing to Co-anda2x means replacing the body and the parts you rely on together, so it makes sense only when a documented Co-anda2x capability covers a job you will repeat.
