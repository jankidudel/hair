Shark lists HD430 as the original US Stone FlexStyle kit and HD430REF as a US Certified Renewed kit. The HD430 page gives an itemized box. The HD430REF page describes two curlers, with other parts appearing under add-ons. That difference matters when you are checking a used or renewed listing: one code has a full maker box list to check against, and the other confirms only part of what comes with it. The diffuser is a third question, answered by its own part page rather than by either box.

What Shark lists for each code

The details come from SharkNinja US’s HD430 listing, HD430REF listing and XSKHD4DA diffuser page. The HD430 heading says Stone and its Product Details say Beige; the HD430REF Product Details say Beige.

Check a renewed listing piece by piece in the Used Multi-Styler Checklist with HD430REF. It starts from the two confirmed curlers and doesn’t borrow the HD430 box. For how these codes relate to the rest of the range, see Shark FlexStyle models compared.

The diffuser part is a separate check

The XSKHD4DA Curl-Defining Diffuser page lists HD400, HD435, HD440, HD430REF, HD435REF and HD432PK. It names HD430REF and doesn’t name HD430. The HD430 page still offers the same diffuser under Add-ons & Accessories. Those are two different signals, and only the part’s own list is fit evidence, so for HD430 the exact fit of XSKHD4DA stays unconfirmed against that list. Read Shark FlexStyle diffuser compatibility before ordering one.

Worked case: a buyer who needs a diffuser. You want to diffuse. Neither listing confirms a diffuser in the box. On HD430REF the part list names your code; on HD430 it doesn’t. Ask Shark US to confirm the pairing for an HD430 before you rely on the add-on.

What to ask when a listing is incomplete

The HD430REF page shows parts such as the paddle brush, concentrator and oval brush under Add-ons & Accessories. An add-on is something you can buy separately; it isn’t evidence that the piece is in the renewed kit. Ask the seller to list every piece and show it in one photo.

Ask for a photo of the rating label, with the serial number covered, so you can see whether the unit is HD430 or HD430REF. The two codes have different evidence behind them: a full box list for HD430, and a partial description for HD430REF. Ask the seller to confirm the unit switches on and the controls respond, as a separate question from what is included. Keep the answers with the listing until the sale is complete.

Worked case: a box photographed with an unlabelled second barrel. The photo shows one 1¼-inch curler clearly and a second barrel without a visible label. Both HD430 and HD430REF list two 1¼″ curlers. Ask for a photo of the second piece with its marking, so you know it is the second curler and not a different attachment.
