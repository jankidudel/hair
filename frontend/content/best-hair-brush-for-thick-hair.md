<p>Thick hair can make a brush feel ineffective when the head is too small or the pins are too rigid. A useful brush should make sectioning and smoothing easier, not turn a quick routine into a struggle.</p>
<h2>For everyday smoothing</h2>
<p>A paddle brush is often a practical starting point for long or dense hair because it can cover larger sections. Choose a head size you can still steer comfortably around the back of your head.</p>
<h2>For detangling</h2>
<p>Flexible pins and open spacing can make controlled passes through sections easier. When hair is wet, use a tool intended for wet use and begin at the ends. See <a href="/guides/hair-brush-vs-comb">Hair Brush vs Comb</a> for a simple comparison.</p>
<h2>Choose by job, not just density</h2>
<p>A styling brush may be useful for a blow-dry, while a detangling tool is better for knots.
