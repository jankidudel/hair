Quick answer

Find the dryer model before removing the filter cover. Use the manual for that model to check which part moves, what can be cleaned and whether the instructions permit water on any removable component.

For the two models covered here, the UK manuals agree. On the original Supersonic HD07 and the Supersonic Nural HD16, the filter cage sits at the bottom of the handle. Unplug the dryer and let it cool, rotate the cage anti-clockwise, pull it down and away, clean both the cage and the filter mesh with a lint-free cloth or soft brush, then rotate it clockwise to lock. Neither manual allows water on the filter mesh.

Identify your model first

The model code is on the rating label and in the manual. The HD07 manual covers the Dyson Supersonic hair dryer. The HD16 manual covers the Dyson Supersonic Nural hair dryer, which has Nural sensors and a capsule light in the middle of its airflow opening. If your label shows a different code, such as a professional edition or the Supersonic r, use that model’s own manual rather than these steps. Supersonic HD07: step by step

  1. Unplug the dryer from the mains and let it cool.
  2. Hold the handle firmly and gently rotate the filter cage anti-clockwise.
  3. Pull the cage down and away. Use a lint-free cloth or soft brush to remove dust and debris from the cage and the filter mesh.
  4. Do not use water on the filter mesh and do not press hard on it.
  5. Rotate the cage back into position until it aligns, then clockwise to lock before use.

Supersonic Nural HD16: step by step

  1. Unplug the dryer from the mains and let it cool.
  2. Hold the handle firmly and gently rotate the filter cage anti-clockwise.
  3. Pull it down and away. Clean dust and debris from both the cage and the filter mesh with a lint-free cloth or soft brush.
  4. Do not use water on the filter mesh and avoid too much pressure.
  5. Rotate the cage back into position until it aligns, then clockwise to lock before use.

Worked example: your HD16 shows three flashing white LEDs mid-session. You unplug, let it cool, remove the cage, brush lint off the mesh, lock the cage back and switch on. Both manuals say that if all three red lights continue to flash, contact the Dyson Helpline and do not turn it back on.

What the lights tell you

Both manuals say the LEDs flash continuously when the machine needs attention, starting with cleaning the filter.
