Which handling pattern fits?

The 2136U manual describes a drying nozzle to remove excess moisture before styling, then different attachments for lift, smoothing or a curl. This suits someone willing to change the head to change the job. The conical attachment requires the supplied heat glove as instructed; it is a different handling step from brushing through a section. Confirm the full current box contents on the seller listing, because bundles can change.

The 2764U manual describes placing its brush under a section, lifting near the roots, moving along the section and turning the styler slightly at the ends. That format keeps the operation centered on one brush. The manual does not describe a powered rotation control, so do not choose it on that assumption.

Preparation and care differ in useful ways

Both manuals tell the user to remove excess moisture and section the hair before styling. The 2136U instructions specify starting at about 80% dry and describe the attachment release buttons. The 2764U instructions also describe rough drying longer or thicker hair before brush styling. These are model instructions, not a universal starting-moisture rule for all hot-air brushes.

For either model, switch off, unplug and let the appliance cool before cleaning. The 2136U manual describes clearing debris from the rear filter with a soft brush. The 2764U manual describes twisting off its rear filter before brushing debris away and replacing it until it clicks. Do not transfer one filter procedure to the other model. Neither UK listing alone proves the exact rated voltage or suitability for another country's mains supply; check the appliance rating plate and manual before travel.
