# Cold Water for Hair: A Styling-Routine Guide

A cooler final rinse is a simple preference some people enjoy as part of their routine. If it feels comfortable, try it after shampooing and notice whether it fits your styling process.

Keep the focus on comfort and the look you want to achieve. Your towel, brush, and styling products will usually have a bigger effect on the finished style.
