Choose by how you want to hold the section and how often you style hair that is already dry. Compare the comb and mode options on the Shark with the wet-to-dry and dry-only instructions for the ghd model. Both tools combine airflow with plates, so the useful differences are in how each mode is arranged and what comes in the Shark box.

Quick answer

If you mostly straighten straight after washing, either tool’s wet mode is built for that job; choose the Shark if you want its three interchangeable combs to guide the section. If you mostly refresh lengths that are already dry, compare the two dry modes: both switch the airflow off and finish with the plates.

Worked choice: straightening after washing

Suppose you wash, towel-dry and straighten in one go most days. Both tools are designed for that wet stage. The practical question is how you want to guide each section through the plates. The SilkiPro box gives you three combs, described as Wide-Tooth, Gentle and Precision, which change how the section is held as it enters. The Duet Style record does not list a comb set, so the section goes straight between its plates. If you like a comb to organise the section, the Shark covers that; if you would leave combs in the drawer, the difference disappears.

Worked choice: refreshing dry lengths

Suppose instead you straighten on day two or three, on hair that is already dry. Here you would use each tool’s dry mode. The SilkiPro dry mode stops the airflow so the plates do the finishing. The Duet Style’s Shine Shot is likewise a no-air plate mode, and ghd limits it to dry hair. In both cases do not use the dry-only mode on wet hair; switch to the wet mode for that. Because the dry modes work in a similar way, base the choice on handling, box contents and price.

Before you order

  • Confirm the Shark code. Comb inclusion is recorded for HT401UKBL1 only; another HT400UK-series code can be packed differently.
  • Confirm the ghd product is the Duet Style and the colour or edition you expect, then read its manual for the wet and Shine Shot sequences.
  • Check today’s price and stock at the same retailer; neither was recorded on a comparable basis for this page.
