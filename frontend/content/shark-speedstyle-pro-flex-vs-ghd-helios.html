Choose which attachment you normally reach for before comparing these two dryers. The Shark SpeedStyle Pro Flex HD542UK (UK) in Mocha Silver has a folding handle and a box that lists four styling attachments, including a concentrator and diffuser. The black ghd Helios (UK) comes with the maker’s contoured nozzle. ghd lists a diffuser separately and specifies an adapter ring for Helios. Start with the dryer you would use for your usual routine, then compare storage, the cord and the attachments you would actually keep by the mirror.

Quick answer

The Shark SpeedStyle Pro Flex HD542UK (UK) box includes a concentrator, diffuser, brush and finishing tool, with a folding handle and a 2.44 m cord. The black ghd Helios (UK) comes with a contoured nozzle and a 3 m cord, and its diffuser is sold separately with an adapter ring. Choose by the attachments you would use and where you would store the dryer.

Separate the included diffuser from a compatible extra

An included diffuser and a compatible one answer different questions. Shark lists the DefrizzFast Diffuser in the Mocha Silver HD542UK box, so it arrives with the dryer. For Helios, ghd sells its professional diffuser separately and says to fit the adapter ring for use with Helios. That is a fit statement for a named part, not an inventory of the Helios box.

If you already own a diffuser from another dryer, start with its part number and the connection. How do you check whether a diffuser fits? lists the details to look up before you assume a part moves between bodies.

Work through two dryer routines

If your routine is mainly concentrator-led: Compare the two included nozzles first, the Turbo Concentrator for HD542UK and the contoured nozzle for Helios. Then compare the documented cords, 2.44 m against 3 m, with the distance from your socket to the mirror.

If you diffuse and need compact storage: Start from the HD542UK included diffuser and folding handle, because both are documented for that exact box. If you are considering Helios, add the separate ghd diffuser and its adapter ring to the list, and check the space you have against the listing before buying.
