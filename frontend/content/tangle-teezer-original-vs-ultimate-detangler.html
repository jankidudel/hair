Choose the standard Original if you want to grip the brush body directly and prefer its palm-held shape. Choose the standard Ultimate Detangler if you want a handle for wet or shower detangling. Tangle Teezer describes the Original for wet and dry use, so a handle is not a requirement for every wet routine. This page compares only the two standard Tangle Teezer brushes, not the separate Wet Brush brand.

Quick answer

Both standard brushes use the maker’s two-tiered teeth, and both UK listings carry the same keep-away-from-heat warning. The documented difference is how you hold the brush and where the maker positions it. The Original is a handle-free body that the listing describes for wet and dry hair. The Ultimate Detangler adds a handle that the listing says keeps it from slipping out of wet hands, and it is positioned for wet hair and the shower. Choose by the grip you will repeat most often, not by which name sounds stronger.

Choose by the action you repeat

Think about the one detangling action you do most often, then test the grip on a manageable section. A brush is easier to use when your hand is not fighting it, and that is easier to judge on one section than on a whole head.

Start each check at the ends and work upward in small passes. Stop at resistance and separate the knot with your fingers rather than pulling the brush through. These checks describe handling only. Neither maker listing establishes a measured difference in force, pulling, breakage or tooth flexibility between the two standard brushes, and nothing here transfers a claim from another Tangle Teezer version to these two.

If you already own one of the two, a second brush is worth it only when it covers a step the first does not suit. A handle that helps in the shower does not make the Original unusable there, and a palm-held body you like for dry hair is not a reason to replace a handled brush that already works.

FAQ

Can I use the Original on wet hair?

Yes. Tangle Teezer’s UK listing describes the Original for wet and dry hair. The handle on the Ultimate Detangler is a grip choice, not a requirement for wet use.

Can I use the Ultimate Detangler on dry hair?

The checked UK listing positions it for wet hair and the shower and does not describe dry use. That is not a stated ban; if dry detangling is your main step, the Original’s own description covers it.

Are the teeth the same?

Both listings describe two-tiered teeth with longer detangling teeth and shorter smoothing teeth. Neither publishes tooth measurements, so this page does not claim they are identical or different in feel.

Can either brush be used with a hair dryer?

Both checked listings say to keep the brush away from heat. Use a brush whose own instructions allow dryer use for blow-drying.
