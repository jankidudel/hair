Choose the Dyson Supersonic Travel HD19 if automatic voltage selection or reuse of a supported Supersonic attachment solves a problem on your trip. Choose the ghd Flight+ if a folding handle and a case in the selected box matter more and you are comfortable setting its voltage selector before plugging in. In either case, check the socket, supply and appliance label for where you will stay. If your current dryer and its kit already meet those requirements, a second dryer may add little to your packing list.

Compare what you will actually pack

The Dyson examples are the UK Prussian Blue/Rich Copper and US Ceramic Pink/Rose Gold HD19 listings. The ghd examples are the black Flight+ listings on ghd UK and ghd US. Check the actual plug shown or stated for your order, particularly when buying from a marketplace or outside your home market. The US Flight+ manual describes a polarised plug with an ALCI safety device. Check the plug supplied with the UK Flight+ and each regional HD19 on the actual unit or with the seller before choosing an adapter.

Swipe sideways or use the arrow keys to compare the two dryers.

Work through the room setup

Make two checks before you pack. First confirm the supply at your destination against the exact appliance label and manual. Then confirm that the physical plug fits the socket or that you have a suitable adapter as specified by the maker. For Flight+, follow the instructions for making the selector change before connecting the dryer. The US Flight+ safety guide excludes voltage converters. Dyson's automatic selection still leaves a separate plug and adapter check. The dryer specs guide explains how to read the label fields when you have the dryer in hand.

Decide which accessory earns its space

If you travel with a brush and usually use a nozzle, compare the two supplied nozzle setups and the space each packed dryer takes in your bag. If you usually diffuse, the HD19 makes sense to consider when Dyson documents your existing attachment family and you have room for the separate diffuser. Its box list gives you a concentrator, so plan for the diffuser as its own item. For a trip where you only need the supplied nozzle, the Flight+ folding handle and case may simplify what you need to carry.

Compare the complete setup for the trip you actually take. Put the dryer, confirmed attachment, plug adapter if needed and storage next to each other. This comparison gives you a way to choose by setup. Drying time, noise and how each handle feels in use would need separate measurements.
