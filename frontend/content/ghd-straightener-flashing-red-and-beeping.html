Quick answer

A flashing red light can indicate failsafe mode, according to ghd UK. Switch the styler off and unplug it. Check the exact model’s support instructions. If the light keeps returning or the behaviour differs from the manual, stop using the styler and contact ghd.

Match the pattern to the model

When it happens What to check
You have just turned the styler on Use the manual for your model to identify its normal warm-up and ready signals. If the signal matches the manual, wait for the ready indication before styling.
The styler has been idle Check the model’s automatic sleep instructions. On the standard Chronos, ghd documents sleep after 10 minutes of inactivity. Other models can use a different timer and signal.
The light flashes red during normal use or returns after power-up Follow ghd’s UK red-light support guidance for your model. Switch off and unplug while you check.
Your ghd Unplugged flashes red when charging Charging is a separate check. Use the Unplugged instructions and the charger guidance for that model.

What to tell ghd

Note the model, the light colour and whether the alert happened at startup, after a period of inactivity or during a pass. If you have it, include the purchase date and describe any guidance you already followed. This helps separate a normal status sound from a fault requiring support.

Keep the housing closed and leave repair work to the manufacturer’s service route. For other tool symptoms see styling tool troubleshooting. If you are still shopping for a standard styler, see ghd Chronos vs Platinum+.

Identify the tool before interpreting the beep

Find the exact name on the styler or its paperwork. Note whether it takes mains power during use or is the cordless Unplugged model. Write down where the light appears and what you were doing just before it started. Switching on, returning after leaving the tool idle, charging and moving through a section each give the support team a different starting point. Use the owner guide for the named model to look up the sequence you observed.

For example, if you walk away from a Chronos and return after the documented sleep period, start with the Chronos sleep instructions and look at the signal they describe. If red flashing starts while you are styling and the appliance stops behaving as expected, follow the red-light support route with the styler switched off and unplugged. A video or forum post about another ghd generation can use the same words for a different set of indicators. Use your model and the task you were doing to narrow the result.

Gather useful details for a support request

Write one short timeline. Start with whether the styler had just been switched on or had already reached its ready state. Then describe the red light and beeps as you observed them. Include whether the issue returned after a check that ghd explicitly advised for your model. If you have a receipt or order email, keep the date and full model handy so support can match the purchase.

If you are switching between the product page and the help page, confirm that the names refer to the same model. Standard Chronos, Chronos Max and Unplugged have different entries. If the owner instructions leave you unsure of what the light means, leave the styler unplugged and ask ghd to interpret the signal for your model.
