Quick answer: a tail comb gives a narrow end for parting and usually finer teeth for close control. A wide-tooth comb gives more open spacing for moving through a section. Choose by the action you need, not by the word “comb” alone.

Choose the task before the material

For a clean part, place the tail where the line should start and guide it through the section, then lift the hair to either side. The teeth can tidy the edge. For a section with knots, start near the ends using a wide-tooth comb suited to that wet or dry routine; work in smaller sections rather than pushing tightly spaced teeth through resistance. Hair length and density change how large a section is comfortable, but do not dictate one universal tooth spacing.

What to check on a real comb

Inspect tooth tips and seams for rough edges, look at spacing along the full comb and decide whether the handle is easy to grip when damp. Check the maker's material and cleaning instructions. A metal tail can be precise, but do not assume it is suitable near powered or hot tools; keep any comb away from exposed heated parts unless the exact model instructions allow that use. Avoid a rigid universal “best” material claim.

If your main choice is between open teeth and a wider flexible head, use the wide-tooth comb versus detangling brush guide. For temporary section hold after parting, see sectioning clips. This page owns the parting-versus-detangling decision rather than another general brush comparison.

Use both only when the jobs are separate

A parting comb can also tidy the hair beside a part, but its close teeth may be inefficient for working through a larger tangled section. A wide-tooth comb can make a rough part, yet its wider edge is less precise when you want a narrow line. For a routine that needs both a defined part and detangling, two inexpensive formats may each do one job well. For a routine that needs only one job, adding another comb is unnecessary. If you are comparing specific products, check tooth finish, grip and whether the tail is comfortable and permitted near the tools you use.

After parting, keep the unused hair aside with an appropriate clip. A comb tail is not a holding device. If you need a soft, imprecise part, your fingers may already be adequate.
