Use the comb that the dryer maker lists for your model. Prepare and detangle a manageable section before styling. If the teeth catch, stop and reduce the section or change the preparation before making another pass. A wide-tooth comb attachment guides hair through moving air so you can stretch or shape a section without a separate brush; it is a different job from diffusing, which spreads air to keep a curl pattern.

Quick answer

Fit the comb your dryer maker lists for your model, detangle a small section first, draw the dryer from roots to ends with the teeth in the hair, and let the ends slide free. If it catches, stop and make the section smaller rather than pulling through.

Check you have the right comb

Dryer comb attachments are model-specific. Two examples show what an exact listing looks like:

  • Dyson’s UK Supersonic wide-tooth comb support page names the comb and its role. It is not a complete fit list or operating sequence, so check your exact Supersonic manual.
  • Shark’s US SpeedStyle Lift & Stretch Wide-Tooth Comb XSKHD3WTCA lists HD300, HD301, HD331, HD332, HD341HRG, HD341HWH, HD352 and HD331REF, and says it is compatible only with the SpeedStyle HD300 series.

If your model is not on a comb’s list, it is not established that it fits. For buying, see comb attachment vs concentrator to decide whether a comb is the right attachment at all.

The technique in five steps

  1. Fit. Attach the comb as your manual directs, with the dryer off, and check it is seated before switching on.
  2. Prepare. Detangle the section first, by hand or with a detangling tool, so the comb is not doing that job.
  3. Section. Take a section you can see through and hold. Clip the rest away.
  4. Move. Place the teeth near the roots and draw the dryer slowly down the length, keeping the teeth in the hair and the air moving with you.
  5. Release. At the ends, let the section slide out of the teeth rather than flicking it, then check it before another pass.

Heat, airflow and starting state come from your exact model’s manual. If the manual gives no setting for the comb, start with the lowest settings it allows and change one at a time.

Comb, concentrator or diffuser?

Use the comb when you want to stretch or lengthen a section as it dries and your dryer maker lists one for your model. Use a concentrator with a separate brush when you want a smoother, brush-led finish. Use a diffuser when you want waves or curls to keep their pattern with less disturbance. Changing attachment mid-routine is fine: for example, comb the lengths, then diffuse the crown.

A worked section

Take a section at the back of the head about as wide as the comb’s row of teeth. Detangle it with your fingers. Fit the comb, set the dryer to the starting setting your manual gives, and place the teeth just below the roots. Draw down over a few seconds, keeping the teeth in contact. At the ends, slow down and let the section slide free. Feel it: if it is still damp in the middle, make one more pass; if the ends caught, detangle before the next section instead of repeating the pass.

For the whole blow-dry around this step, use how to blow-dry hair. If frizz appears as each section dries, see frizz after blow-drying.
