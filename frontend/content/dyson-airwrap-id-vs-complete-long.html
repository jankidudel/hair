The Dyson Airwrap i.d. Straight+Wavy (UK) in Ceramic Patina/Topaz and the Dyson Airwrap Complete Long (UK) in Nickel/Copper both come with curling barrels, styling brushes and a presentation case. The meaningful differences are the exact barrels and brushes in those boxes and the app-assisted curling sequence Dyson describes for the newer listing. If you already own a working kit, put the attachments you reach for most at the top of the comparison. A current owner and a first-time shopper can use the same list, with one extra step for the owner: check which parts you actually have.

Check your regular attachments

For the Airwrap i.d. Straight+Wavy (UK), Dyson describes Bluetooth pairing, a six-question hair profile and an automated styling sequence in the MyDyson app. If that sequence is your main reason for considering another unit, decide when you would use it. Write down the curling step you would want to run with the app, then check whether either of the listed barrels is the one you want for that step.

For the Airwrap Complete Long (UK), start with the parts you already use. If you regularly reach for the firm smoothing brush or the long 30 mm barrel, those names appear in its box. The named Airwrap i.d. Straight+Wavy (UK) list shows a soft brush and a different barrel mix. Check separate documentation if you plan to reuse a head. Two ownership cases

If you already have a routine built around a long barrel: Pull out the barrel you use and check its full name. The Airwrap Complete Long (UK) lists two long barrels. The Airwrap i.d. Straight+Wavy (UK) lists a 40 mm barrel and a conical one. Decide whether you want that second shape, the app sequence, or both. This keeps an attachment decision separate from the decision about a different handle.

If you are choosing your first kit: List one drying head, one brush and one barrel that match jobs you expect to repeat.
