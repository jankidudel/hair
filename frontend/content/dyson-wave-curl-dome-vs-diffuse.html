Dome and Diffuse are two configurations of the Dyson Supersonic Wave+Curl diffuser. The difference you can check is the removable prong insert. Take it out for Dome mode and fit it back in for Diffuse. The part is Dyson 973924-01, and Dyson UK’s support pages describe both the mode change and which Supersonic dryers the diffuser fits. This guide keeps to those two questions. It doesn’t claim one mode gives a better result; that depends on your hair and how you dry it.

How to swap the insert

Switch the dryer off and let the diffuser cool before you handle the insert. Lift the prong insert out of the diffuser for Dome, or seat it back in for Diffuse, following the steps on Dyson’s support page. The insert is part of the diffuser, so keep it with the part when you store it or buy one second-hand.

If you are buying a Wave+Curl diffuser second-hand, ask to see the prong insert as well as the diffuser body. Without the insert, only Dome is available, and the listing should say so. Ask for the part number if it is visible, and compare it with 973924-01 before you rely on Dyson’s fit statements for this part. A diffuser for another Dyson product is a different part with its own fit list.

Worked case: Supersonic Nural owner choosing a mode. Your Ceramic patina/topaz Supersonic Nural came with the Wave+Curl diffuser; the Dyson UK listing names it in the box alongside the Flyaway attachment, Styling concentrator, Gentle air attachment and Wide-tooth comb. Choosing a mode is a matter of fitting or removing the insert; nothing else on the dryer changes.

Fit and Attachment Learning

Dyson’s support tool matches 973924-01 to the Ceramic patina/topaz Supersonic Nural. Check it in the Hair Dryer Attachment Finder with the Nural preset. Dyson separately describes the part as physically fitting previous Supersonic dryers, and reserves Attachment Learning for the Supersonic Nural. Those are two claims with different scopes. Physical fit is not the same as the dryer recognising the attachment.

Worked case: previous Supersonic owner. You own an earlier Supersonic, such as the HD07 in Dyson’s UK manual. Dyson’s family statement covers physical fit on previous Supersonic dryers, but no exact match for your model is published on the pages we hold, and Attachment Learning stays with the Nural. Confirm your model with Dyson before buying the part. The statement does not cover Supersonic r, Supersonic Travel or Airwrap.

The dryer family matters because Dyson’s attachments are built for one body type. If you are deciding between a dryer and a multi-styler, read Dyson Airwrap vs Supersonic. Use the UK HD16 manual to confirm that your dryer is the Nural.
