The ghd Chronos Curve Conical Wand has two published end diameters, 28 mm and 23 mm. The Chronos Curve Max Wand has a 38 mm barrel. Comparing the Max to each end of the taper shows exactly how those published diameters differ: the Max is 10 mm wider than the Conical Wand’s wide end and 15 mm wider than its narrow end. Those two numbers describe the barrels; they don’t predict a finished style. ghd’s UK listings give neither wand a heated barrel length, so length isn’t compared here.

What each published number measures

On a taper, each figure is the diameter at one end of the barrel, and the barrel narrows between them. On a straight wand, one figure covers the whole barrel. So the honest comparison is end by end: 38 mm against 28 mm is a 10 mm difference, and 38 mm against 23 mm is 15 mm. Draw them at the same scale in the Hair Tool Size Visualiser, taper vs cylinder. Because no length is published, the drawing shows the end circles rather than a barrel silhouette.

Both are wands: you wrap the hair around the barrel yourself, with no clip. If you’d rather have a clip holding the ends, compare the ghd Classic and Grand clip tongs at 26 and 32 mm.

Compare with a wand you have

With the tool off and cool, measure across the heated barrel at each end. If the two ends are different, you have a taper, and each end is its own figure. The curling iron barrel size guide explains diameter, usable length and taper in more detail.

Worked case: matching an old tapered wand. Your old wand measures about 28 mm at the wide end and 23 mm at the narrow end. The Conical Wand’s published ends match those two numbers. The Max Wand doesn’t have a narrow end at all; it is 38 mm the whole way along.

Worked case: a 32 mm straight barrel against the taper. You own a 32 mm cylindrical wand. It is 4 mm wider than the Conical Wand’s 28 mm end and 9 mm wider than its 23 mm end, and 6 mm narrower than the Max Wand. Enter 32 mm in the size visualiser to see all three at the same scale.

It also helps to decide which end of a taper you care about. If you usually start a curl at the wide end, compare that end. If you finish sections around the narrow end, compare that one too. A single cylinder like the Max gives you one diameter along the whole barrel, so there is only one number to match. Write both ends of any taper you own on a note before you shop, so you’re comparing numbers rather than impressions from a photo.

Listings from other sellers sometimes round or restate these numbers. Check the ghd UK listing for the exact wand before relying on a different figure.
