A hot-air brush dries and shapes at the same time, while a straightening brush is typically a finishing tool for dry hair. The useful choice depends on where each fits in your routine.

Hot-air brush

Choose this for a brush-led dry with shape and airflow together.

Straightening brush

Choose this when your hair is already dry and you want a more direct smoothing step.

Compare the finish

Think about volume, bend and the time you want to spend on sections.
