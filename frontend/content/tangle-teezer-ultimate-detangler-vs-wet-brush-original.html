Lock the variants before comparing

The UK Ultimate Detangler Matte page describes longer teeth for detangling and shorter teeth for smoothing, a matte finish and a handled shape. It advises removing loose hair, occasional washing with warm water and mild soap or shampoo, air drying, and keeping the brush away from heat. The US Original Detangler Frost Black page describes flexible IntelliFlex bristles, an ergonomic design and wet or dry use. Its promotional breakage and comfort claims are presented with its own test caveat; they do not constitute a direct test against this Tangle Teezer variant.

What might change the choice

Someone who prefers the feel of alternating tooth lengths and a textured matte grip has a documented reason to examine the Tangle Teezer. Someone who prefers a flexible-bristle layout and a familiar handled brush may examine the Wet Brush. Neither source gives a consistent comparable measurement of pulling force, pain, breakage or ease across these two exact items, so do not rank them on that basis. If you already own a detangler that works comfortably and is in good condition, there is no evidence that switching brands will improve the result.

Both products have variant families. “Ultimate Detangler” also appears in naturally curly and other designs; Wet Brush has mini, pro and heat-oriented lines. Do not move a heat permission, size or care direction between them. The Tangle Teezer Matte restriction is explicit. For the broader question of whether any detangler can be used near a dryer, see heat-use permissions. For a broader shortlist, see detangling brush selection.

Use a simple in-hand check

Where possible, hold the exact brush and inspect grip, head reach and whether its contact pieces remain intact; do not infer a universal winner from a product photo. Start detangling from the ends and work through manageable sections as the maker permits. If bristles or teeth are bent, loose or difficult to clean, the replacement guide is more relevant than a brand comparison. The brush versus comb owner covers a different tool-choice question.
