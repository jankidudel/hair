<p>Boar bristle and nylon brushes do different jobs. Choosing between them is less about declaring one universally better and more about deciding how much smoothing, detangling and styling control you want.</p>
<h2>Boar and mixed bristles</h2>
<p>Boar or mixed-bristle brushes are commonly chosen for gentle everyday smoothing. A mixed design adds nylon pins, which can make it easier to guide the brush through more substantial sections.</p>
<h2>Nylon pins</h2>
<p>Nylon pins vary greatly in flexibility and spacing. A flexible design can be useful for detangling, while a denser layout may give more directional control. Check whether a brush is intended for wet use before using it in a damp routine.</p>
