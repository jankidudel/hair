Quick answer

Buy for the shortest section you style regularly. Start with ghd Mini if you want slim plates and a preset. Compare Cloud Nine Slim Iron if you want similar access with selectable temperature. If your bob fits a standard head comfortably, use ghd Gold as the wider reference before buying a mini.

Compare the total head around the plates as well as the plate width. A narrow plate can still sit in a body that crowds the section. If cord position affects the angle you can reach, check the handle and cord arrangement on the exact model.

Do an unplugged reach check

Find a small front section and a regular section near the ends. Hold your cool, unplugged iron beside each one. Check whether you can position the plates without approaching skin, then whether you can turn your wrist for the shape you want. If only the fringe is awkward, a slim iron can make sense for that job. If both sections already fit, look at controls before changing width.

Use the plate-width guide for measurement and the UK adjustable-temperature shortlist if heat selection is the main reason to buy.

Choose for one short section and one longer section

Start with the fringe if that is where the width of your current iron makes the tool difficult to place. Compare the slim head options on the outside of the section with the tool cool and unplugged. Consider the angle your hand and elbow need to reach. If both heads give you the clearance you want, choose next by whether you prefer a fixed temperature or want to select a setting as the maker directs.

Now look at a longer piece around the sides or at the ends of a bob. If it fits across your current plate and you can turn it comfortably, a standard iron may already suit the bulk of your routine. If the fringe needs a smaller tool but the longer pieces are straightforward, decide whether you want to use one iron for both or keep your current tool for the lengths. Compare which approach you would reach for on an ordinary morning.

Check the shape beyond the plate width

When comparing product photos, look at how far the housing extends past the plates and where the hinge falls in your grip. Check where the controls sit when you turn the tool for an end bend. A narrow plate dimension helps with the shortlist. The full shape gives you more information about how the tool fits by the face or behind the ear. Follow the exact styling directions when using a heated appliance, including the required starting state for the hair.

On a local offer, confirm the exact model name and the stated plate size or narrow-head description. If you are replacing a wider iron because the setting is difficult to use, check the controls separately. Changing width may leave the control problem where it was.
