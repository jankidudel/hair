Choose a fixed control if one documented preset and simple operation suit you; choose an adjustable model when you intend to select among permitted settings. A manufacturer sensor or monitoring claim is a different feature from a user control. Neither type of straightener comes with a universal personal temperature recommendation.

What the control actually lets you do

The US ghd Chronos, Platinum+ and Gold list a preset 365°F styling temperature. Their descriptions discuss different sensing or monitoring designs, but none offers a temperature dial on these exact listings. The US BaBylissPRO BNT4172T lists five digital positions. That gives the user a choice among named settings; it does not prove precise internal regulation at every point on the plate.

Keep the indicators separate

A selected number is the user request; a ready light indicates the appliance says it has reached a ready state; an inactivity shutoff describes when it powers down without use. These answer different questions. The US Chronos page states a 10-minute inactivity shutoff; Platinum+ and Gold pages state 30 minutes. A longer delay is not automatically better. Some users prefer shorter shutoff, while others prefer more time before restart. Confirm the exact model, market and manual rather than moving a feature between ghd generations.

If you frequently change settings because the maker's directions or your routine call for it, adjustable control offers a real option. If you always use the same permitted position and value a simpler panel, fixed may be enough. Neither path establishes a safer result from specifications alone. If your current iron has controls you use and its plates handle your sections, a new sensor name does not establish a need to upgrade.

What to compare next

Choose the control approach first, then compare plate width and handling in the width guide. If your shortlist is Chronos and Platinum+, the exact-model owner covers their other distinctions. Read the appliance instructions before using any setting, and leave undocumented accuracy or hair-type performance unknown.

Choose the next control, not a number

If a retailer shows a digital display, ask whether it shows the selected setting, a readiness state or a measured plate temperature. These are different quantities and a display can show only one. An exact model manual can establish which buttons select a position and whether it remembers the previous choice. Without that documentation, do not assume a screen means continuous adjustment. The useful purchase difference remains whether you can intentionally select among settings allowed by the maker.
