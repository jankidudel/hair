# How Often Should You Wash Your Hair?

There is no single schedule that suits every routine. Consider how you style your hair, the products you use, and how fresh you want your look to feel between wash days.

Try a simple rhythm for a couple of weeks, then adjust it when your routine changes. A gentle shampoo and a thorough rinse can make wash day feel more manageable.
