What the technology names do and do not establish

ghd describes Chronos as “motion-responsive” and Platinum+ as “predictive,” and publishes separate branded test and marketing claims for each. Those claims have different comparison conditions. They do not establish that one will style your sections faster, produce a safer result or be less damaging under the same routine. The documented control distinction is real: the standard models use a preset temperature while their described monitoring systems differ. If you need a dial, neither page supplies it.

Both models have rounded bodies and floating plates described by the maker. That can inform how you expect to handle a turn or bend, but it is not a measured ergonomics result.
