Hot-air-brush care involves more than the bristles. The exterior, barrel air outlets and an accessible filter can have different maintenance instructions. Identify the exact model before choosing a cloth, cleaning brush or removal step, and keep the electrical appliance out of an improvised ordinary-brush wash.

Quick answer

Switch off, unplug and let the tool cool as instructed. Remove accessible loose hair using the permitted method, then follow the model's separate directions for the exterior, air outlets and filter. A detachable head is not automatically washable, and a visible filter is not automatically removable.

Identify which appliance you have

Confirm the full name and generation, including whether the tool supplies airflow. A dry-hair heated smoothing brush has another care owner: how to clean a straightening brush. A non-electrical round brush belongs to ordinary hair-brush cleaning.

Check the maintenance instructions and supplied accessories for the airflow model you actually own. A similar barrel shape does not establish the same filter cover, attachment joint or liquid permission. Keep those differences visible before beginning.

The Airsmooth/Duet Blowdry comparison distinguishes two named brush appliances before care is chosen. A fixed Airsmooth head does not become detachable because an Airwrap part has a similar name. Keep this guide's named Duet Blowdry care instructions with that exact appliance and check the other model's own maintenance directions.

Two support pages explain one named model

The UK ghd Duet Blowdry aftercare page describes an unplugged, cooled appliance, no immersion, a permitted exterior wipe and use of its supplied cleaning brush for rear-filter and barrel-outlet care.

The separate UK Duet Blowdry cleaning support article identifies the rear filter and explicitly says it cannot be removed. That is a useful example of why a generic remove-the-filter instruction is unsuitable. Keep these directions with that model rather than transferring them to every hot-air brush.

Work through external care without forcing access

  1. Read the exact maintenance section and gather only the permitted cloth or cleaning accessory.
  2. Complete shutdown, unplugging and cooling before handling the barrel or outlet area.
  3. Remove loose hair through the documented method, working on accessible strands without forcing the bristles.
  4. Clean the specified external contact and housing surfaces as directed.
  5. Follow the separate outlet and filter instructions, including any explicit limit on removal.
  6. Complete the required drying, reassembly and storage steps before use.

Do not remove a cover, unscrew the body or rinse an attachment because another model allows it. If a part does not release through the instructed action, stop and check the maker's support rather than forcing access as a maintenance step.

Respond to what remains after permitted care

If visible hair or residue remains, ask about the next compatible method instead of escalating to a blade, abrasive cleaner or direct spray. If airflow or operation remains abnormal, use the manufacturer's service guidance. Cleaning does not establish that a damaged connection or internal fault is repaired.

Inspect the cord, attachment seating and accessible surfaces before the next use. Follow a maker's care schedule where supplied and respond to actual visible buildup; there is no shared few-uses or weekly interval established for every model.
