Quick answer

Choose SpeedStyle HD332 if you mostly dry or diffuse and want the diffuser in that US box. Choose FlexStyle HD430 if you regularly use airflow curling barrels, brushing attachments or a dryer concentrator in the same routine. The HD430 package in this comparison does not list a diffuser.

What is your dryer missing?

If you already own a dryer and use it mostly with a diffuser, write down the one job it cannot do. If that job is only an occasional brush-led finish, compare an ordinary brush or separate styler before replacing the dryer. If you expect to switch between brush styling and curling barrels every week, look at the FlexStyle layout and which two attachments you would actually use.

Check the model code on the box before ordering. SpeedStyle Pro, SpeedStyle Pro Flex and UK FlexStyle kits have their own bundles. See SpeedStyle Pro vs Pro Flex for the UK dryer comparison, or Airwrap i.d. vs FlexStyle for the US HD430 comparison with Dyson.

Decide what each tool would replace

If you currently diffuse your hair most wash days and only rarely use a brush to finish, write down those two jobs in that order. The named SpeedStyle box includes the diffuser and two finishing options. When shopping for that routine, check the HD332 listing before comparing the price of a FlexStyle bundle with a diffuser sold separately. Confirm what you would use for drying and finishing and base the price comparison on those parts.

If you usually rough-dry and then use a distinct tool for curls or brush shaping, walk through those steps with the HD430 kit list. Decide whether you would actually swap to a curling barrel after drying or mainly use the oval and paddle brushes. The body’s rotating design lets you consider the dryer and styling positions as part of the same purchase. Look at the order in which you would use each head rather than giving every included piece equal value.

If you mainly want an oval brush for a blowout, compare Shark FlexStyle vs Revlon One-Step Volumizer Plus before deciding which attachments you would use.

Read the bundle name and the box list together

The family name on a seller page may be the same for several bundles. On the exact US listing, find the model code next to the price and compare the named diffuser, concentrator, brushes and curling barrels. When two listings differ, use the one with a clear accessory list or ask the seller to confirm which head comes with its box. If you already own a Shark attachment, check its part code against the intended new body before you include it in your buying plan.

A useful final check is to list the two heads you would keep within reach. If one is missing from your chosen box, add the separately sold accessory to your comparison only after verifying fit and availability.

For a gift, ask which job the recipient already does most often. Someone who uses a diffuser weekly and seldom curls has a different starting point from someone who alternates between a blowout and airflow curls.
