What the different technology words cannot decide

ghd's Gold page describes two heat sensors and the Platinum+ page describes predictive monitoring. The two pages contain branded performance statements made under their own conditions. They do not establish how the two exact tools would compare on your hair in the same independent test. A sensor is not a selectable temperature control. Both sources describe preset styling rather than a personal setting decision; see fixed versus adjustable controls if that is your actual choice.

Gold's plate guard is useful only if its exact box includes it and the maker's storage directions fit your routine. Platinum+'s longer listed warranty may matter when choosing a new US unit from an authorised seller, subject to the actual terms and proof of purchase. A longer inactivity delay is not a reason to prefer one here because both are listed at 30 minutes. For travel, check the rating and plug of the physical unit despite Gold's universal-voltage statement; do not generalise another generation's support wording.

Before replacing either tool, separate a control problem from a handling problem. Write down the stage that is awkward: selecting a temperature, positioning the plate around a short section, routing the cord, or storing the cooled tool. Then identify the published feature that would change that stage. The shared preset and plate label do not, by themselves, resolve a request for adjustable heat or a different working width. If your concern is grip or balance, a specification sheet cannot settle it; check whether the seller permits the kind of inspection or return you would need before purchasing.

Who might choose each

Choose Gold when a fixed 1-inch format and its documented guard and two-year warranty fit the purchase, without paying for a technology name you do not need. Choose Platinum+ when its listed ownership terms and design are what you value; do not claim its sensor system will necessarily yield a better finish. If you want a shorter inactivity shutoff, compare the separate Chronos versus Platinum+ owner. If width is the real issue, use the plate-width guide. Check the exact US retail bundle at purchase because colour and package extras can change independently of the tool.
