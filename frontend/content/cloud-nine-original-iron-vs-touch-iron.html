Choose between the current Original Iron's broader heat selection and the Touch Iron's two-setting, plate-activated control system. Check the exact model before using an older review: the UK listings also state different voltage ranges.

Quick answer

Consider the current ordinary Original when you need its broader heat selection. Consider Touch when its two-setting, plate-activated arrangement answers your task. A working iron that already offers suitable access and control may need no replacement. Compare the actual UK label before travel, because the two listed voltage ranges differ.

Identify the actual generation

The ordinary current Original, an older Original generation, Contouring and Cordless are separate records. Match the complete product name and appliance label before borrowing a review’s specifications. This page compares the current UK ordinary Original with Touch. It gives no basis for regarding the ordinary Original as cordless, or for assigning its controls to a similarly named Contouring model.

Compare the selected controls

The current Original listing describes eleven temperature choices from 100 to 200°C. Touch offers 165°C and 195°C with plate-activated controls. Both name one-inch plates, so a larger count of settings answers a control preference here. It does not establish a measured finish advantage. Consider which selections you use repeatedly and whether an existing tool already provides them.

Use the tool’s indicated setting

Touch’s main feature text and FAQ reverse the two- and three-tap mapping. This comparison therefore gives no tap-count recipe. Use the instructions supplied with your exact tool and its indicated setting, asking the manufacturer if those remain unclear. Combining the two conflicting passages would leave a reader unsure which temperature was selected. The two documented temperatures can still be compared without inventing the sequence.

Understand hibernation

The current Original description gives thirty minutes of inactivity, while Touch gives thirty seconds. These are manufacturer descriptions of different behaviour, with neither a measured response-time result nor a replacement for switching off and unplugging as directed. Check the exact tool’s restart and storage instructions. A faster hibernation description alone cannot determine which tool is more convenient during your own sectioning pauses.

Check the label before travel

The ordinary Original lists 100–240 V and the selected UK Touch lists 220–240 V. Record the rating of the actual appliance before planning a trip. A free dryer shown in a promotional bundle has its own electrical rating. Original’s voltage cannot be carried onto that gift. Destination voltage, plug arrangement and appliance permission must remain attached to each individual item in the bag.

Keep the width decision separate

If the difficulty is reaching or covering a section, compare Original versus Wide and plate width before adding another one-inch candidate. If width already works and the unresolved issue is choosing a setpoint or using plate activation, this Original/Touch comparison addresses that choice. Retention, shine and timed-pass results remain unmeasured here.
