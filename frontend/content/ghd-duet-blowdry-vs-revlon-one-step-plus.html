Quick answer

Choose ghd Duet Blowdry if you want its heated brush design with one styling setting and it fits the sections you normally take. Choose Revlon Volumizer Plus if you use selectable settings or want to remove the oval head for storage. If you already have a Plus that gives you the finish you want, identify what a change would improve before buying another brush.

Decide what would change on wash day

If you reach for a lower setting for face-framing pieces and a different setting for the rest, the selectable controls are a real difference. If your current brush setting stays the same through a whole blowout, ask instead whether the new tool improves your grip or the way the section feeds onto the brush. A short, cool handling check can tell you more about that than a wattage comparison.

Compare the same US model and condition if you are weighing the price. The two listings establish the design and included parts. They give you less information about how quickly your usual section will dry or how long a finish will last.

Compare two different wash-day routines

If you usually shape the front first and use a single setting throughout, see how the ghd brush head fits into that first pass. The on/off layout may suit you if you rarely reach for another setting. If you change settings between the front and the back, check where the Revlon selector sits while holding the handle. Imagine reaching it with the brush away from your hair between sections. The useful difference is whether you can reach the control when you need it and return to the same grip.

Then check the shortest layer. With your existing brush cool and unplugged, take the section you normally use and picture how far the ends would have to turn around each head. A head that works well for long layers may crowd the same spot on a fringe. If you style both areas, decide which job your main brush needs to handle and whether you already have another tool for the smaller section.

Decide how much the detachable head matters

If you store your brush in an open place after it cools, the removable head may matter less to you than the control layout. If you have a narrow drawer, compare the space for the handle and head separately. Look up the exact US variant and check which components come with it. If you make the same blowout successfully with a working Plus, that is a useful starting point for deciding whether a different head or a simpler control is worth buying.
