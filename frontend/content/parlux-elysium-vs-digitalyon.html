Quick answer

Compare the controls and the nozzle or diffuser you would actually use. If you already own a Parlux attachment, look up its exact fit before assuming you can carry it over to a new dryer.

Parlux describes the Elysium as a compact professional dryer with 3 speeds and 3 temperatures, giving 9 combinations, and the Digitalyon as a dryer with 2 speeds, 4 temperatures and a separate cold-air button. The Digitalyon page names two nozzles in its Standard version, a regular nozzle and a slim “straightener effect” nozzle, and a second version packed with the MagicSense diffuser. The Elysium page names its AirFusion nozzle and pairs the dryer with the MagicCompact diffuser. Choose by the controls you will press every day and the attachment you will keep on the dryer.

If you already own a Parlux dryer

Start with the attachment you would want to keep. A MagicSense owner moving from an Alyon or older Digitalyon should note that the Parlux diffuser compatibility guide lists MagicSense for Alyon, Digitalyon and Advance, and keeps Elysium with the MagicCompact. In practice, a returning owner who diffuses every wash day can keep a MagicSense routine by choosing the Digitalyon, while a move to the Elysium means budgeting for its own diffuser.

Next, compare the control habit you already have. If you rely on a dedicated cold-air button to set a section, the Digitalyon page names one. The Elysium page describes 9 speed and temperature combinations but does not name a separate cold button, so check its instructions before assuming the same finishing step. Readers comparing two older Parlux bodies can use Parlux Alyon versus Digitalyon for motor and pack differences.

If this is your first Parlux dryer

Decide which attachment does most of the work. A smooth blow-dry with a round brush usually keeps a nozzle on the dryer, so compare the AirFusion nozzle with the Digitalyon’s standard and slim options. Parlux says the slim nozzle dries a wider band, close to the width of a common brush, which matters if you work in broad sections. If you diffuse most of the time, compare the MagicCompact and MagicSense offers you can buy locally, because a pack that already includes the diffuser changes the real price.

Then think about storage and handling. Parlux calls the Elysium compact and its motor a mini-motor, while the Digitalyon is described as a full-size professional dryer that has been lightened and rebalanced. Without published weights, the honest comparison is size and grip: if possible, hold both, or check returns terms so you can try the handle with your own brush.

Keep the filter in your routine

Both pages put filter care in view. The Elysium uses a removable sponge filter that Parlux describes as washable; the Digitalyon pairs a patented filter with a Hair Capture System at the intake. Follow the exact manual for removal and drying before refitting, and use our hair dryer filter cleaning guide for the general sequence.

A simple decision path: keep a MagicSense diffuser and want a cold button, look at the Digitalyon first. Want a compact body and are happy to buy the MagicCompact, look at the Elysium first. In either case, confirm the version and its attachments on the retailer page before checkout.
