Define the section before choosing a pack

A clip holding an entire upper layer has a different job from one parking a small piece beside a parting. Estimate the amount you want to remove from the working area, then check whether the mechanism closes without forcing it. Overall clip length is an incomplete fit field. The opening, where the jaws meet and the amount of hair caught at the hinge can all change the handling decision.

Framar describes Gator Grips for big sections and points to the Super Sectioner's elastic band for updos or cuts. Those statements help you recognise the intended design. Choose a closure you can operate

Check the lever reach and whether you can open and close the mechanism with your usual hand position. Keep fingertips and loose ends clear of the hinge.
