Choose the Airstrait if you want to include drying and straightening in the same routine. Consider Corrale CoolShine if you already dry your hair separately and want a plate straightener for dry styling. Start with the state your hair is normally in when you use the tool. This page compares the UK Airstrait in Plum/Copper with the UK Corrale CoolShine in Ceramic pink. Comparisons with the original cordless Corrale stay on the generation guide.

Quick answer

If you style from damp and want a straight finish without a separate dryer, look at the Airstrait. If your hair is already dry when you straighten, the corded, dry-use CoolShine fits where a flat iron already sits. Count which stages each one would remove from your routine.

Start from the state your hair is in

If you usually pick up a straightener after a separate blow-dry or after letting hair air-dry, the CoolShine fits where your current tool sits. The Airstrait changes the routine: you start from damp hair and finish drying and straightening with the same tool. That can remove a stage, but only if a straight finish is what you want most days.

Try writing your usual routine as stages, for example “towel, dryer, flat iron”. If the Airstrait would replace two of those stages with one, it is solving a real problem. If you still need the dryer for other looks, count what changes before paying for it.

A practical check before choosing

  1. Note whether your hair is wet or dry when you normally straighten.
  2. Note whether you use a dryer for anything other than preparing to straighten.
  3. If the answers are “dry” and “yes”, the dry-use plate tool matches your routine. If they are “wet” and “no”, the wet-to-dry tool may remove a stage.

Follow the starting-state and mode directions supplied with the exact unit. A two-arm outline does not make the Airstrait a flat iron, and a plate straightener is not a drying tool.

If you already own one of them

Owners of a CoolShine who want to skip a separate dry would be adding a wet-to-dry tool, not replacing a like-for-like straightener; the CoolShine still covers dry-hair styling between washes. Owners of an Airstrait who want a plate tool for touch-ups already have a documented Dry mode to try first. A second Dyson straightener earns its place only if it covers a stage the first one does not.
