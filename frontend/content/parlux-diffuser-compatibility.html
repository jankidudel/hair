Start with both names: the dryer model and the diffuser model. MagicSense and MagicCompact are different attachment decisions, and an adapter supplied with one does not make every Parlux pairing compatible.

Quick answer

For Alyon, Digitalyon or Advance, the cited MagicSense listings document direct fit. For Elysium, use the separately named MagicCompact. If your dryer is Ethos or an older model outside the table, verify the exact local part and approval before ordering. An approved diffuser you already own may cover the task without replacing your dryer.

Find both model names

Read the dryer label and the diffuser listing together. A seller using only “Parlux diffuser” has left the essential pairing unresolved. Copy any suffix and local part reference from the item you will actually buy. The fit table combines the named manufacturer statements. It supplies no all-years approval list for every similarly shaped Parlux outlet.

Read the fit table before considering the adapter

The English and Italian MagicSense pages name Alyon, Digitalyon and Advance for direct fit. Their general adapter wording also carries exclusions. Keep those exclusions attached to the statement when checking another body. The US MagicSense offer has a sparse description, so its existence alone cannot supply a complete US model matrix or resolve a conflicting local package.

What the adapter statement establishes

Use this decision sequence. First identify MagicSense or MagicCompact. For MagicCompact, check for Elysium. For MagicSense, look for a documented direct-fit entry, then read the exact adapter statement and exclusions for an otherwise eligible model. If the maker has not established your pairing, stop at an unresolved fit question. Buying an adapter does not establish approval.

Why the Ethos wording needs care

The Italian MagicSense product page explicitly includes Ethos in its direct-fit list. The English list differs. That is useful evidence with a visible source-language boundary. Ask the local supplier to identify the actual diffuser and its approved Ethos pairing. Leave a missing language entry visible rather than regarding an omission as a new exclusion or assuming every regional offer is identical.

Check the selected package

A diffuser accessory package and a dryer bundle are separate purchase records. An adapter supplied with a MagicSense accessory does not establish that a dryer box includes that diffuser. Put the selected dryer, diffuser and adapter on separate lines before comparing an offer. If you already have a documented compatible diffuser, compare the remaining drying task before buying another body.
