<p>The decision is simple once you separate drying from styling. A hair dryer is the more flexible all-round tool. A hot-air brush is a styling shortcut that may suit people who regularly want a smooth, brush-led finish.</p>
<h2>Choose a hair dryer if</h2>
<p>You want quick basic drying, use a diffuser, share a tool with different hair types or prefer separate brushes and styling tools. A dryer works on very wet hair and is generally easier to adapt to different routines.</p>
<h2>Choose a hot-air brush if</h2>
<p>You often dry and style in the same session, like a smoother blow-dry look and are happy to work in smaller sections. It can reduce the number of tools in your routine, especially for short to medium lengths.</p>

<p>Many people get the best result by using both: a dryer to remove most moisture, then a hot-air brush to shape and smooth. That is not essential, though. Choose one based on the routine you will actually maintain.</p>

<h2>The bottom line</h2>
<p>Buy a dryer for versatility. Buy a hot-air brush for a convenient styled finish. The right answer is the tool that removes the most friction from your normal routine.</p>
