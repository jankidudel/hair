Read the model code first and open the matching Shark instructions. Switch the FlexStyle off, unplug it and let it cool before carrying out the filter care described for that model. The steps below are Shark’s procedure for the UK HD440UK and HD420UK series; other FlexStyle families have their own documents.

Quick answer

For an HD440UK or HD420UK: switch off and unplug, let it cool, gently pull the lower cover off, brush debris from the mesh down towards the cord with a dry cloth or a clean toothbrush, clear the cover and slide it back until it clicks. Shark describes doing this monthly.

Find your model code

The code is printed on the unit’s rating label and on the box. Shark’s UK codes include a suffix for the market and bundle, so a box code such as HD446UK can identify a package rather than a different body. If your code is not HD440UK or HD420UK, or a package code you cannot match to one of them, look up your own model’s support page before using the steps below.

Shark’s filter care for HD440UK and HD420UK

These steps follow the Shark UK HD440UK/HD420UK support article as recorded on 18 November 2026.

  1. Switch off, unplug and cool. Remove any attachment and let the unit cool fully on a dry surface.
  2. Find the air intake. On these models the filter mesh sits behind the lower cover, on the cord side of the handle.
  3. Remove the cover. Gently pull the lower cover off. This is the part Shark lets you handle; do not open any other part of the body.
  4. Clean the mesh dry. Brush lint and dust from the mesh down towards the cord using a dry cloth or a clean toothbrush.
  5. Clear the cover. Remove any lint caught in the cover itself.
  6. Refit before use. Slide the cover back on until it clicks. Do not run the unit with the cover off.

For stuck debris, follow only the extra step in that support article. Its permission applies to these models and is not carried over to other bodies.

What not to do

  • Do not clean the filter while the unit is plugged in or warm.
  • Do not add water, sprays or cleaning liquids unless your model’s own instructions say so.
  • Do not push tools through the mesh or open the body beyond the lower cover.
  • Do not apply another model’s method, interval or stuck-debris step to your body.

If the unit still runs hot, shuts off repeatedly or smells of burning after the documented clean, unplug it and stop using it until Shark support has advised you.
