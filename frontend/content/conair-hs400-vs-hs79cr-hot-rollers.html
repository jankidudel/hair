Choose the US Conair HS400 if your plan needs more rollers and the smaller diameters in its twenty-piece inventory.

Quick answer

Choose HS79CR when its eight larger rollers cover the sections you want to set. Count pieces by diameter before comparing the size of the case. Plan for the specific diameters you want

The example assumes six sections each need a separate 1¼-inch roller at the same time. Under that assumption HS400 covers the plan and leaves two spares at that diameter. HS79CR supplies four, so it leaves two of those sections uncovered. Its four 1½-inch rollers are different planned curves. You could redesign the section plan, but should record that change instead of silently counting the larger rollers as equivalent.

Count the closures alongside the rollers

Each occupied roller also needs its original supported closure. The selected HS400 listing describes heated plastic clips and HS79CR describes super clips. These are kit inventories, not approval to exchange a closure between kits. If a clip is missing, use the exact kit label and Conair support to identify an approved spare. Similar-looking plastic shapes and the same nominal roller diameter cannot establish fit on their own.

Read readiness from the exact model

A powered case tells you that the unit is connected. A ready dot or colour change gives the separate cue documented for that set. Follow the model's directions for initial heating, loading, setting and removal. The current HS79CR page describes orange rollers becoming white when ready and changing back while cooling. The linked booklet is labelled HS79, so confirm which guide accompanies your purchased revision before transferring detailed operating steps.

Check usable width before heating the kit

The counted diameters do not measure the usable cylinder width or the section you can wrap. Make a section plan using the actual roller working area and the path for the ends. If a section overflows the ends, split it and recalculate how many rollers you need. That may change which inventory covers the plan. CombReport supplies no head-coverage guarantee, curl-retention comparison or universal heating time for these two sets.

Record the exact choice

Put the roller count, diameter and supported clip in a single checklist before setting up the case. Mark occupied pieces and spares for each diameter, then revise the plan if working width requires another section. This lets you compare a purchase with an actual set plan while leaving heating and release timing to the exact instructions.
