Compare the two boxes by the work you want them to do. The Air Wand box covers drying, smoothing and shaping with a brush. Check the FlexStyle box if you expect to use airflow curlers or another specific attachment. The comparison below goes job by job, so you can see which box already includes a part for each one.

Quick answer

If your routine is drying, straightening and a brushed blowout, the BaByliss AS6550U box covers it. If you also want airflow curls or a diffuser in the box, the Shark HD446UK includes both. Choose by the two jobs you would do most, not by the number of attachments.

Handle movement

The FlexStyle has a hinge: Shark says a single twist rotates it between a hair dryer and a styling wand, so you dry with the body angled and style with it straight. The Air Wand records describe drying from the handset itself without a fitted head, then fitting the straightening attachment or oval brush for styling. If you dislike changing grip between drying and styling, try both shapes in a shop before choosing.

Shopping example: two jobs

Suppose you want to dry quickly on weekdays and diffuse waves at weekends.

  1. AS6550U: drying is covered. A diffuser is not in the box; BaByliss sells one for the Air Wand range, but its listing does not name AS6550U, so ask BaByliss to confirm fit for your code before buying it.
  2. HD446UK: drying and diffusing are both covered in the box. You also get curlers and two brushes you may or may not use.

Now swap the second job for a smooth, brushed blowout. Both boxes include an oval brush, so the choice comes down to handling, price and whether the extra Shark parts earn their place.

If you already own attachments

Attachments are not interchangeable between the two brands. A BaByliss part fits only the bodies BaByliss names for it, and the same applies to Shark. If you already have parts for one system, list what you use before switching; for the BaByliss side, the Air Wand AS6550U vs AS6553U guide covers the extra smoothing brush and spare parts.
