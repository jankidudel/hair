Dyson Supersonic and Shark SpeedStyle sit in the premium hair-dryer conversation for different reasons. Both aim to make drying and styling faster and more controlled than basic dryers, but the best fit depends on what you value in day-to-day use.

The comparison that matters

Do not compare only headline claims. Check the exact version, included attachments, heat settings, airflow controls, warranty and current price. Bundles can differ substantially between retailers and countries.

Choose Dyson if

You prefer its design, specific attachment set or the broader Dyson styling ecosystem. It may also appeal if you value a familiar premium brand and are comfortable with its price positioning.

Choose Shark if

The supplied attachments and control layout better match your routine, or if its current price makes the package more compelling. Shark’s hair-tool range has several variants, so make sure you are comparing the SpeedStyle rather than a different Shark styling system.

What neither comparison can decide for you

No online guide can tell you whether a handle feels right in your hand or whether an attachment fits your styling technique. Buy from a retailer with a sensible returns policy, and avoid seeing a dryer as a promise of a specific cosmetic outcome.

The bottom line

Compare the exact bundle in front of you. The better choice is the dryer whose attachments, controls and price fit how you actually dry your hair.
