Quick answer

Identify the Airwrap before removing a part. The Airwrap i.d. has a removable cage and fixed mesh. Dyson gives different care instructions for those two surfaces. Use the exact support material for older Airwrap models or Co-anda2x.

Clean the Airwrap i.d. cage and mesh

  1. Switch off, unplug and allow the styler to cool. Follow Dyson’s instructions to slide the cage down the cord and open it at the split.
  2. Gently brush the fixed mesh with the supplied C-brush or a soft toothbrush. Keep water and cleaning products away from the mesh.
  3. Loosen debris from the detached cage with the brush. Rinse that cage and brush under warm running water. Dyson’s UK i.d. instructions describe using a small amount of clarifying shampoo on the brush for the cage, then rinsing it away.
  4. Remove excess water from the cage and let it dry fully. Refit the cage securely before switching the styler on.

If the indicator stays on

Dyson’s i.d. support identifies flashing white as a request to clean the filter. For flashing red, it directs you to clean the filter and contact support if the care has already been done. Use the model’s own warning guidance for other Airwrap generations. If a warning returns after the approved care, stop using the tool and contact Dyson.

Know which surface you are looking at

For Airwrap i.d., the support instructions distinguish the removable outer cage from the mesh that stays on the machine. Identify both parts before you start. Then read all four steps once so you know when the detached cage can be rinsed and when it needs to dry. If you are unsure where the cage splits or how it should sit when it is fitted, use the illustration or video for your exact model. Keep the appliance switched off and unplugged during the care procedure.

If you own an older Airwrap or Co-anda2x, start at the separate support link in the table. Open the manual for the body you have and check the sections for filter maintenance and indicators. An article with a familiar product name may show a different generation. The model and the part identified in Dyson’s instructions give you the basis for deciding whether those directions apply.

Record what happened before the warning

Note the light colour and when you noticed it. Include whether the machine stopped during use and whether you had already carried out the approved maintenance. If you do carry out the procedure, check the cage is fully dry and securely fitted before returning to normal use. If the indicator comes back, give Dyson the model, what you saw and which steps you followed. That record helps support determine how to handle the warning on your exact machine.

For example, a flashing white light on an Airwrap i.d. takes you to the i.d. filter-care instructions. If the same model flashes red after that procedure is already complete, the support page gives you the contact route. For another generation, compare the light and circumstances with its own documentation first.
