This comparison is limited to the US Dyson Airwrap i.d. multi-styler and dryer and the Shark FlexStyle 4-in-1 Air Styling & Drying System, model HD430. They are multi-stylers, but a listing for either family is not proof that every attachment is in every bundle.

Compare products
Sources checked October 2, 2026: Dyson US Airwrap, Dyson US Airwrap i.d. support, and Shark US HD430. The table reports manufacturer-published bundle information, not independent performance testing.

Compare the exact bundle

Both product families have variants and retailer bundles. Before buying, list the attachments included in the exact package: curling barrels, brushes, concentrators, diffusers and any specialist smoothing attachment. Do not assume a feature shown in one review is included in every current bundle.

Choose by routine

If you mainly want a smooth blow-dry, assess the brush and concentrator attachments first. If you want curls, assess the barrel sizes and how comfortable the technique looks for you. If you have curly hair, check whether the included diffuser is the version you need.

Limitations that can change the decision

Neither name alone answers whether a diffuser, wide-tooth comb, particular barrel, storage case or voltage arrangement is included. The HD430 product page lists a 120 V, 1,300 W unit; do not assume that makes it suitable for travel outside its documented electrical use. Dyson lists Airwrap i.d. accessories and support separately, so confirm the exact bundle rather than inferring contents from an accessory page.

Verdict by user type

Choose the Airwrap i.d. only when its exact official bundle contains the attachments you will use. Choose the HD430 when its documented five-piece in-box set fits your routine. There is no evidence-based overall winner from these published specifications alone: people who need a diffuser or a different brush should compare the actual bundle and any separate accessory cost before deciding.

Keep expectations sensible

These are premium styling systems, not automatic replacements for good technique. Hair length, density, preparation and sectioning all affect the result. Buy from a retailer with a practical returns policy if possible, especially if ergonomics are important to you.

The bottom line

Choose the system whose attachments you will genuinely use. A smaller, well-matched bundle is better value than a larger kit that spends most of its life in a drawer.
