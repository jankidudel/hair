Quick answer

Note which light flashes, how many lights you see and when it happens. Then identify your dryer. The standard Supersonic and Supersonic Nural have different features, so match the pattern against Dyson’s instructions for your model. Switch the dryer off and unplug it before any filter care described in the manual.

Match the indicator to the dryer

What you see What to do
Three flashing white lights on a standard Supersonic covered by Dyson’s UK support page Dyson directs owners to clean the filter. Use the linked care instructions for the exact model.
Three flashing red lights on a standard Supersonic covered by that support page Dyson also directs owners to check and clean the filter. If the lights remain after the approved care, switch off and contact Dyson.
A light visible at the rear of a Supersonic Nural Identify the light’s position and whether Scalp Protect is running. Use Nural’s own support flow to distinguish a status light from a fault or maintenance prompt.
A pattern you cannot match, or lights that return after approved care Stop using the dryer and contact Dyson with the model and pattern.

What to collect before contacting support

Write down the model and which lights appeared. Record whether the pattern started on power-up, during normal drying or after the dryer stopped. Add whether airflow changed and whether you already carried out Dyson’s approved filter care. This gives support enough detail to choose the right model-specific next step.

For a general explanation of removable cages and fixed mesh, see how to clean a hair dryer filter. If the dryer runs but the air is unexpectedly cold, use the cold-air troubleshooting guide.

Make a short record before looking up the light

Write down the model as it appears on the dryer or your purchase record. Then record whether the light is on the controls, at the rear of the dryer, or in another position. Count the lights you saw and note whether they flashed together. If you remember changing the heat, switching a mode or setting the dryer down just before the light appeared, add that detail. You can record what happened from memory while the dryer is switched off and unplugged.

For example, three white lights on a standard Supersonic lead you to Dyson’s standard dryer support and its matching filter guidance. A light at the rear of a Nural during a mode you deliberately selected leads to the Nural model page. The useful first distinction is the model and location of the indicator. If the brief description in a search result leaves either detail unclear, open the full support record for your dryer rather than applying the first set of instructions you see.

Move from the support article to the next step

Read the care section for the identified model before touching the filter. Check which part Dyson expects the owner to handle and how Dyson describes normal reassembly. If you already used the approved procedure and saw the warning again, record the date and what the lights did afterward. Give those details to Dyson with the model. Tell support if airflow changed or if the appliance stopped during use. A short sequence of what happened will help them identify the relevant support flow more readily than a description such as “a red light appeared.”

If you cannot identify the dryer, ask Dyson to help match the model before carrying out filter maintenance. Keep the appliance switched off and unplugged if the warning remains unresolved.
