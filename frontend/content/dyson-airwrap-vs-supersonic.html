If you want one tool for wash day, start by listing what you do between getting out of the shower and putting the tool away. The Dyson Airwrap i.d. Straight+Wavy (UK) in Ceramic Patina/Topaz lists a drying attachment alongside curling barrels and styling brushes. The Dyson Supersonic Nural (UK) in Ceramic patina and topaz lists a diffuser, a concentrator and a wide-tooth comb among its dryer attachments. Both named kits give you a way to dry hair. The useful question is whether your regular routine depends on an attachment that comes with only one of the two.

Quick answer

Choose the Supersonic Nural (UK) if your routine depends on a diffuser: its box lists the Wave+Curl diffuser, and the Airwrap i.d. Straight+Wavy (UK) box lists none. Choose the Airwrap i.d. if drying usually runs straight into a brush or barrel, because its box adds a fast dryer, two barrels and two brushes. If you already own one of them, check whether it covers your usual drying step before you buy both.

Decide what the drying step needs to do

Dyson’s own comparison describes the Airwrap for “drying, curling, smoothing and hiding flyaways” and says fast drying “is what Dyson Supersonic™ hair dryer does best.” In the Airwrap i.d. Straight+Wavy (UK) kit, the route from drying to styling includes a barrel or brush already in the box. Dyson describes a Bluetooth connection to the MyDyson app, a six-question profile and an automated styling sequence. That gives you a specific reason to read about the connected controls if you plan to use the curling barrels often.

The Supersonic Nural (UK) listing gives you a different starting point. Look at the dryer heads you would actually pick up. If your routine uses a diffuser some days and a concentrator on others, both are listed in this exact box. If diffuser fit matters because you own another dryer or attachment, find the model and attachment before comparing sizes. See how to check whether a diffuser fits for that separate check.

Two wash-day cases

If you mainly diffuse curls: Write down the diffuser as a requirement before the other attachments. The Supersonic Nural (UK) lists the Wave+Curl diffuser. Dyson does not list a diffuser in the Airwrap i.d. Straight+Wavy (UK) box. If the drying attachment and barrels sound useful for another routine, give that routine its own place on your list and decide how often you would use it.

If you dry and then reach for a brush or barrel: Start with the fast dryer in the Airwrap i.d. Straight+Wavy (UK) box, then identify which of the included brushes or barrels you would reach for. If you already own tools for those steps, note which of them you would still keep nearby.
