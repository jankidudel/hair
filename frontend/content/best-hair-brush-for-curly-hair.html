There is no single correct brush for curly hair. The more useful choice is between a wide-tooth comb, a flexible detangling brush and occasional styling brushes based on when you detangle and the finish you enjoy.

For damp detangling

Many people with curly hair prefer a wide-tooth comb for its open spacing. A flexible detangling brush can also work well if it suits your technique. Start at the ends and work through manageable sections.

For texture-focused styling

Dry brushing can create unwanted frizz for many people, or a softer, larger look for others. It is not required for curls. A diffuser-oriented drying routine may be more relevant when you want to keep a defined finish.
