A round brush is a styling tool: choose it for the shape you want, not because one barrel size claims to suit everyone.

Match the barrel

Smaller barrels offer more bend and control; larger barrels support a looser finish on longer sections.

Check the handle

A comfortable handle matters when you are coordinating brush movement and airflow.

Use sensible sections

Smaller, manageable sections make it easier to direct a finish without rushing.
