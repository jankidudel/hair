The Hot Tools 24K Gold One-Step (US) and the Revlon One-Step Volumizer Plus (US) both list oval brush heads, with different published size labels and different levels of detail about the controls. Hot Tools gives a 2.8-inch label and a count of three heat settings. Revlon gives a 2.4-inch label, names four heat settings and describes a detachable head with a release button. Start by deciding whether you care most about the head coming off, the named controls or how much room you have around the section you style. The listings answer the first two questions more directly than the third.

Quick answer

Choose the Revlon One-Step Volumizer Plus (US) if you need a head that comes off or named heat settings: Revlon lists a detachable head and low, medium, high and cool. The Hot Tools 24K Gold One-Step (US) lists a larger 2.8-inch oval label and three heat settings, but not whether its head detaches. Neither maker states how its head size is measured.

If the space around a short section is your main concern, figure out what area you need to clear and look for an actual head measurement. Check storage and the settings you want to select

If the head must come off for the place where you want to put the brush, the Revlon One-Step Volumizer Plus (US) listing gives you a documented detachable design. The Hot Tools 24K Gold One-Step (US) maker does not state whether its head detaches. Compare the storage space you have with the tool you plan to buy and keep head removal as an open question where the maker leaves it open.

For controls, Revlon spells out low, medium, high and cool on the Revlon One-Step Volumizer Plus (US) page. Its maker does not state separate speed settings there. The Hot Tools 24K Gold One-Step (US) page lists three heat settings, and its maker does not state their labels. If you are also weighing up the two Revlon versions, see Revlon One-Step Volumizer vs Plus for the comparison within that range.

The listing controls leave another question for anyone who wants to choose heat and airflow separately. Write down the combination you want to select. Then compare it with the published settings and ask the maker about any missing control. This same check helps if you want to replace a brush with a dial you already know.

Work through two buying decisions

If your shortest layers are the difficult part: Write down where you need to place the brush head, then consider the room around that section. You can use the 2.4-inch and 2.8-inch listing labels to identify the two heads. Leave actual clearance undecided until you have the dimensions you need or can check the head yourself.

If the brush needs to fit into a narrow drawer: Put a removable head on your checklist if storing the pieces separately would help. The Revlon One-Step Volumizer Plus (US) meets that check on its product page. For the Hot Tools 24K Gold One-Step (US), leave the removal box blank and check with the maker if that detail decides the purchase.
