Choose the FlexStyle box by the parts you expect to use. A kit number or colour does not tell you whether the diffuser, curlers or case are included. Compare a named fixed kit with the selections you would make in Build Your Own. On 23 November 2026 the three UK listings below differed most in the diffuser, the curler sizes on offer and whether storage is in the box list.

Quick answer

Write down the two or three parts you will use, then pick the box that lists them. On the checked UK pages, HD446UK includes a diffuser and storage case, HD432PKUK has no diffuser, and Build Your Own lets you choose four accessories. Confirm stock and selections before paying.

Availability is recorded separately from contents because it changes daily. On the check date, all three pages showed stock warnings. The box lists are what the maker documents for each code; the stock state only tells you whether you can order that box today.

Bundle code is not an appliance generation

The three codes identify packages. HD446UK is sold as a 5-in-1 with a storage case, HD432PKUK as a limited-edition 4-in-1 in Pearl Pink, and HD400UK-BYO as a 4-in-1 you assemble. None of the checked pages states that its main unit is a newer FlexStyle than the others, so do not read a higher number or a gift-set label as an upgrade. A different system, such as FlexStyle versus Glam, is a separate decision with its own guide.

The HD446UK page also shows the colour as Black in one place and Black/Rose Gold in its product details, and the BYO page lists Black in its specifications but Stone in its details. Match the code on the order confirmation, not the colour name or a photograph.

Example checkout check: diffuser and oval brush

Suppose you need a diffuser for waves on most days and an oval brush for a smoother blowout on others. Work through each listing in order:

  1. HD446UK: both parts are in the box list, along with curlers, a paddle brush and a concentrator you may not need.
  2. HD432PKUK: the oval brush is listed, the diffuser is not. You would need a separately sold diffuser that names your exact body.
  3. HD400UK-BYO: select Diffuser and Oval Brush as two of your four choices, then decide whether the remaining two (for example curlers or a concentrator) earn their place. Confirm the selections on the basket page before paying.

The same method works for any combination: write the two or three parts you will actually use, then find the box that lists all of them. Extras you will not use are not value, and a missing part bought later adds a fit check.
