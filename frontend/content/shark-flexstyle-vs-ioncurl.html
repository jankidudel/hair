If you own a UK original FlexStyle and are moving to IonCurl, Shark UK says the original FlexStyle accessories carry over. Shark separately excludes Glam accessories. If you are buying a new IonCurl part for an older body, or buying a spare from a US listing, start with both model codes. The replacement listings can describe two product families in general copy and still give a narrower list for the exact part. Check the part first, then decide whether a new bundle would give you anything your existing attachments cannot do.

Check which direction you are moving

The UK Shark migration comparison addresses accessories moving from original FlexStyle to IonCurl. This is family-level manufacturer guidance. For the other direction, check the spare itself. The UK pink IonCurl diffuser XSKHD8DAPKUK names IonCurl. Approval for an original FlexStyle body is unconfirmed on that exact page. If your existing styler is Glam, Shark’s UK exclusion applies. The Glam vs original FlexStyle comparison covers the different purchase decision and powered tools.

Look up the exact US replacement part

Find the model on the body, then match the part code and the body codes in the listing. Each US listing below also uses broader HD800 and HD400 wording. The table reproduces the scope of the specific compatibility field. If your exact body is absent, it is not explicitly listed. Ask Shark to confirm the pairing before ordering, even if the family name appears elsewhere on the page. A compatible part and support for a particular control or styling feature are separate checks.

Check the chosen kit separately

The UK pink HD800UKPKBYO lets you choose three attachments. The diffuser is a choice in that configuration, so check whether it is selected and appears in your order. The US HD800 Build Your Own listing shows separately advertised add-ons. Use the contents of your configured order to decide which pieces you will receive. The US lilac diffuser code does not identify the pink UK part or establish what the UK kit includes.

Decide whether to keep the current styler

Start with the attachment you use every time. If your current body works and you only need a replacement for that job, a confirmed spare may solve it. If you are considering a different body, write down the attachments you want to carry over and check the exact pairing in the appropriate market. Then choose the kit based on the remaining jobs. For example, if you already have a confirmed compatible concentrator and want to diffuse, check the diffuser approval and kit selection before counting the concentrate-and-diffuse tasks as covered. Use the multi-styler attachment guide to decide which roles are worth keeping.

A kit list can establish what Shark offers with a configuration. A part listing can establish the approved pairing it names. Neither measures how well a curl holds or how the airflow feels with your section. For an unlisted pairing, supply Shark with the body label and exact spare code rather than trying to seat an attachment that does not fit normally.
