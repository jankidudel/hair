Choose around when you want to straighten. SteamPod 4 is a steam-assisted styling step on dry hair. Airstrait has separate instructions for wet and dry styling. Check the starting state you use most and the setup you would repeat. The two tools also differ in contact: SteamPod presses the section between ceramic plates with steam, while Airstrait shapes it with airflow and no hot plates.

Quick answer

If you want to go from washed hair to straight in one tool, Airstrait is the one of the two with a documented wet mode. If you already dry your hair first and want a plate finish, SteamPod 4 fits after drying, provided you are happy to fill its tank with demineralised water before each session.

Fit each tool into your week

Count which kind of session you do more often. If most of your straightening happens straight after washing, a dry-hair-only tool adds a full drying step before you can start. Airstrait’s wet mode is designed to cover drying and straightening together. If you usually dry with a dryer and straighten later, or mostly smooth day-two hair, the SteamPod 4 fits that rhythm, and the Airstrait would be used in its Dry mode instead.

Setup is the other repeated task. SteamPod 4 needs its tank filled with the specified water: release and lift the tank with its two lower buttons, open the tab, fill to MAX and refit the front before clicking the back into place. Airstrait has no tank, so the repeated task is managing the cord and the mode selection.
