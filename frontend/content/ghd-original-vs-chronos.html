Quick answer

Choose ghd Original if you want the simpler model and a 24 mm plate suits your usual sections. Consider Chronos if you want a slightly wider plate and its motion-responsive heat control. Both use a preset. If you want to select the temperature yourself, start with the adjustable-temperature shortlist.

Would you notice the upgrade?

Take your usual front section and your usual section at the back. With the current tool unplugged and cool, check how much of the plate you already use. If the two sections fit and you can turn the tool for bends, look for a specific remaining problem before replacing it. If you regularly use wider sections and prefer the Chronos controls or handling, check the standard model in that size before comparing prices.

If you are coming from a wide-plate iron, compare physical head width and plate length as well as the 24 mm and 26 mm figures. Those widths tell you about the plates. Clearance also depends on the body and hinge.

Check whether the extra width matters in practice

Put a ruler beside the front and back sections you usually take and look at where the width falls. You can make this comparison without clamping or heating anything. A wider plate gives you a little more working surface across the section. How much of that surface you can use depends on the width of the section, how flat it sits and how you hold the iron. On a fringe or around a short layer, consider the room taken up by the outside of the tool as well as the plates.

If the front works well and the back takes longer, note what causes the delay. If you spend most of your time dividing or clipping hair, a change of plate width may leave the bulk of the work where it was. If a few sections routinely extend past the usable surface, compare those sections with the larger footprint before buying. When you curl or bend ends with the iron, check the space you need to turn it between your hand and the ends of the section.

Make the replacement decision

For a first ghd purchase, start with the plate width, preset operation and how the tool will fit into storage after it cools. Then decide whether Chronos gives you something you would value every time you use it. For a replacement, write down what you want to improve before opening the product listings. Put both UK prices side by side for the same condition and check the included accessories and warranty terms on each offer. If your only concern is selecting a lower or higher setting yourself, move to the adjustable-temperature shortlist before comparing either price.
