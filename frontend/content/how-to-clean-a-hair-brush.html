Cleaning a hair brush is mostly about removing loose hair and keeping the surface pleasant to use. The safest routine depends on its materials, especially if it has a wooden handle or a cushioned base.

Remove loose hair first

Lift trapped hair with your fingers or the end of a comb, working gently around the bristles.

Clean the surface carefully

Use a lightly damp cloth or follow the maker’s care instructions. Avoid prolonged soaking when the brush materials are not designed for it.

Let it dry fully

Place the brush bristle-side down or in a position that lets moisture leave before the next use.
