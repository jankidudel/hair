Quick answer

Choose Shark Glam if the Shark Silki attachment is the reason you are shopping. It combines ceramic with airflow for straightening. Choose the Dyson Airwrap i.d. if you want brush-led shaping and an app-guided curling sequence. Both kits can dry and curl hair. The useful difference is what you can do with the attachments you will actually take out of the box.

This comparison covers the UK Shark Glam HD6051SUK and Dyson Airwrap i.d. Straight+Wavy in Ceramic Patina/Topaz.

Pick the two attachments you would use

For roots and straighter lengths after every wash, start with the Shark Silki attachment and the dryer setup. Check how you would position a short front section, and whether a separate finishing attachment would be useful to you. For a brush blowout and occasional curls, start with Dyson’s round brush and one curling barrel. If you would curl without a phone, check the manual controls before giving the connected sequence extra weight.

Work through a week before choosing a kit

Imagine that you wash your hair twice a week and use a brush on both days, then curl it at the weekend. If the brush finish is already the one you want, compare the Glossi and Dyson round brush before looking at the straightening attachment. Ask where you need the most control. It could be near the front, where there is little room to lift a section, or through the lengths, where you want to keep the hair on the brush until the ends release. Those are different reasons to prefer one shape.

Now imagine you attach your current straightener after every blowout because you want a flatter finish through the lengths. The Shark Silki is worth a closer look in that routine. Read its preparation instructions and decide whether you would actually switch attachments for that pass. If your last step is simply dealing with a few visible flyaways, compare the finishing heads instead. A tool used every wash day deserves more weight in your choice than an attachment you would use once to try it.

Look at storage too. List what would stay on the counter and what would go in the case. If the kit you prefer includes the two heads you would keep within reach, use those as your basis for comparing offers.
