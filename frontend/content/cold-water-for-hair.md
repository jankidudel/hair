<h1>Cold Water for Hair: A Styling-Routine Guide</h1>
<p>A cooler final rinse is a simple preference some people enjoy as part of their routine. If it feels comfortable, try it after shampooing and notice whether it fits your styling process.</p>
<p>Keep the focus on comfort and the look you want to achieve. Your towel, brush, and styling products will usually have a bigger effect on the finished style.</p>
