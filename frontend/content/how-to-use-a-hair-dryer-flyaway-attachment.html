A flyaway attachment is a finishing choice with its own starting condition and movement. It is not simply a concentrator with a different opening. Establish which part you have and whether the intended style is ready for that finishing step before fitting it to the dryer.

Quick answer

Match the attachment to the exact dryer and follow its preparation instructions. For the named Supersonic Flyaway example, begin with dry, straight or straightened hair, let the hair be attracted and guide the attachment along it without pressing down. Do not transfer that movement to a different brush, contact bar or smoothing mode.

Identify the actual finishing part

Read the attachment name and body compatibility together. Flyaway attachment, flyaway smoother and dryer/smoother are not sufficient evidence of identical controls. A part may use a different surface, mode selector or contact action despite sharing a broad finishing purpose.

This guide uses the UK Dyson Supersonic Flyaway attachment support instructions as one example. It does not document every Supersonic generation or the Airwrap Dryer+Flyaway smoother. Keep fit and retail inclusion separate: a part described in support may not be in the box you own.

Complete the required drying and shaping first

The named example specifies dry hair that is straight or has been straightened. That requirement describes the suitable starting state for this attachment; it is not a reason to straighten every hairstyle. If you want to preserve a different texture or shape, choose a method intended for that result instead of adding this step automatically.

For earlier direction and shaping, use concentrator-nozzle use. For deciding between an attached comb and a separately guided brush, use comb attachment versus concentrator. Neither owner's starting state should be borrowed for the finishing part.

Plan the route before applying airflow

Identify the surface area you intend to finish and how you can reach it with the handle in a controlled grip. Keep the inlet clear and follow the appliance's own separation from skin and other safety instructions. A smooth-looking tool surface does not prove that it is cool enough to touch.

Consider where the pass should end. Finishing the upper lengths and deliberately shaping the ends are different choices. Do not keep following the tool towards a new shape simply because there is more hair below the original area you intended to address.

Follow the named movement without adding pressure

Dyson's instructions for this Supersonic part describe attraction near the top of the head followed by slow movement along the length, without pressing down. The same support page notes that the attachment can become hot. Use its handling directions and the actual dryer's instructions when fitting, reorienting or removing the part.

Observe a defined area before deciding whether any further finishing is useful. Do not press harder, increase several settings or repeat indefinitely to pursue an advertised result. Loose hairs alone do not establish why a finish looks frizzy.

Keep the finish optional

A style can be complete without this attachment. If the current drying method already gives the finish you want, record that as the baseline before adding another heated step or buying an accessory. A comparison of exact dryer bundles can establish documented parts and features, but it cannot promise that a particular reader will prefer the finished result.

After use, follow shut-down and cooling directions before storage. Use the exact attachment's care instructions; a smooth exterior does not authorise rinsing a joint or changing its internal parts. For the broader sequence, return to how to blow-dry hair.
