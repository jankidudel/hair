Start this pair with the brush path you want to use. The standalone Dyson Airsmooth and ghd Duet Blowdry are brush-led drying appliances, but their named head designs and heating descriptions differ. This comparison uses the UK Ceramic pink/Rose gold Airsmooth and black Duet Blowdry.

Quick answer

Investigate Airsmooth if its teardrop brush path suits the smoothing or shaping task you repeat. Investigate Duet Blowdry if its elliptical heated-barrel-and-airflow arrangement is the format you want. Confirm reach, release and the exact starting instructions before replacing a dryer-and-brush routine that already works.

Identify the two products correctly

This Airsmooth is a complete standalone appliance, not the AirSmooth2x attachment supplied in a Co-anda2x kit. Duet Blowdry is the ghd brush appliance, not the two-arm Duet Style straightener. Their similar names do not establish the same accessories or operating modes.

Test the shape against the section

Consider the shortest piece you want to style as well as a long section. Can the hair reach the useful part of the brush, follow the intended movement and come away without gathering neighbouring hair? A large-looking head may be workable on one section and awkward on another.

Rehearse the path with an unpowered display model where available. That can reveal access and hand-position preferences; it cannot establish heat performance or a finished result. Do not roll more hair around a head simply because the appliance is marketed for a blowout. Head-size choices help separate a published measurement from the reach test.

Keep starting state attached to the exact mode

A tool that dries and styles can still have a specific preparation requirement. Read what the selected appliance expects before the brush stage, then compare that with the point where you want to start. Avoid a universal dampness percentage or assuming that both products begin at an identical state.

If you mainly want a dry-hair touch-up, check that instruction separately. It is not permission to adopt another appliance's mode or setting. Thermal brush versus hot-air brush owns the broader heat-source distinction, including a manual brush used with a separate dryer.

Include control and care in the shortlist

Inspect the exact switches and manual when a particular control is essential. Do not compare a single option count without establishing whether it includes a cool position or measures a different function. A sensor-frequency claim also does not rank stability or styling performance.

Plan how the tool will be cleaned, cooled and stored. A fixed head does not have to detach for its permitted external care, and a removable part is not automatically washable. The existing hot-air-brush care guide uses model-specific instructions; do not copy Duet Blowdry's cleaning actions to Airsmooth.

Write the task that handling one powered brush would simplify. If you need independent control of dryer and manual brush, those two objects may remain useful.
