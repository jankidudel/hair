Quick answer

Both are oval blow-dryer brushes. Choose based on which head has space to turn your shortest working section and which controls you can reach in your grip. If you need a compact round head, compare the other Drybar sizes. If you want a detachable Revlon head, look at the Plus separately.

Check the three sections you style most

Start at the crown and see whether you can hold the brush at the angle you use without reaching for its controls mid-pass. Then check if a long section lies across the head and still releases easily. Finally, try the shortest face-framing piece with the brush cool and unplugged. If both oval brushes crowd that spot, look at a smaller round shape.

For that choice, read Drybar Double Shot vs Single Shot vs Half Shot. For a detachable brush, see Revlon Original vs Plus. If a dry-hair smoothing mode is the feature you need, see Revlon Plus vs Shark SmoothStyle.

Compare your grip before you compare the power figures

Think through the part of the blowout that takes most of your time. At the crown, see where your thumb would sit if you hold the brush at the angle you normally use. Through the lengths, look for enough room to keep the section on the head until you want to release it. For shorter face-framing pieces, hold your current brush cool and unplugged nearby and see whether you have room to turn and move away. Comparing those motions gives you a way to judge two oval brushes even if their listings measure the heads differently.

Suppose the oval shape already works through the crown and long layers, but you have to move the front pieces around the side of the brush to make them fit. Replacing it with another oval brush may give you a different control layout and still leave the front crowded. Compare the smaller Drybar round options for that part of the routine. If most of the trouble is holding the handle while reaching the selector, keep the two oval choices and check the controls on the exact model photos.

The Revlon One-Step name covers more than one brush. Check the full product title and the control descriptions on the US offer.
