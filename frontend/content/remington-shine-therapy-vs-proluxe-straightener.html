Check the exact Remington codes before comparing the settings. Look first at the temperature you actually use, how to select and lock it, and the plate dimensions each UK model documents. On 24 November 2026 both UK listings gave the same nine-setting range; they differed in the shortcut, the lock wording and a 1.5 mm difference in plate width.

Quick answer

Choose the S8500 if you want a locked setting that stays where you put it and you already know the temperature you use. Choose the S9100 if you would use its PRO+ one-press setting at 185°C regularly. If neither of those matters, the range and plate length are the same, so price can decide.

A familiar setting can matter more than a new feature

If you already straighten at one setting and like the finish, the most useful control is the one that gets you back to it quickly and keeps it there. The S8500 documents a temperature lock and a digital display, which suits someone who sets a number once and does not want to knock it while working through sections. The S9100’s PRO+ shortcut suits someone who already uses about 185°C and wants to reach it with one press instead of stepping through settings.

Neither feature changes the range. Both start at 150°C and stop at 230°C. Remington’s description of PRO+ and OPTIheat is the maker’s account of how the S9100 manages plate heat; this page did not measure either iron. Use the lowest setting that gives you the finish you want, and follow the manual for the exact code you buy.

Plate width: what 1.5 mm changes

Both plates are 110 mm long. The S9100’s 25 mm width is 1.5 mm narrower than the S8500’s 26.5 mm. That is a small change; it may matter only if you regularly work close to a fringe or short layers. For a wider view of how plate width relates to section size, use the flat-iron plate-width guide.

Check you have the right PROluxe

Remington uses PROluxe for several products, so confirm all of these before ordering:

  1. The listing or box shows the code S9100.
  2. The product is a straightener with two plates, not a dryer or styler.
  3. The name does not include “You”; PROluxe You is a separate line with its own controls.
  4. The plate size reads 110 × 25 mm and the range reads up to 230°C, matching the UK listing above.

If any line does not match, compare that product on its own listing rather than using this table.
