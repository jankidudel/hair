The UK ghd Chronos Curve Classic Tong has a 26 mm barrel, while the Grand Tong has a 32 mm barrel. Both are clip tongs. If you are replacing a tong you already own, measuring its working barrel gives you a direct comparison. That is the useful way to read the 6 mm gap between the two: against a barrel you already know, not as a promise about the curl you’ll get. ghd’s two listings publish the diameter and the clip format, but neither gives a heated barrel length, so this guide doesn’t compare length.

What the two clip tongs share

On the listings, the two tongs share the Chronos Curve name, the clip format and the 185°C setting. The published difference is the barrel diameter. A 6 mm wider barrel means a larger circle for each wrap of hair. How that looks depends on your hair’s length, how much you hold in the clip and how long you leave it, so the diameter is the starting point rather than the answer.

A clip tong holds the end of the section against the barrel. A wand has no clip and you wrap the hair yourself. If you’re undecided between those two formats, compare the tongs with the ghd Conical and Max wands.

Match a tong you already use

When the tool is off and cool, measure across the heated barrel with a ruler or calipers, with the clip closed and away from the tip. Write it down in millimetres. For what diameter, usable length and taper each answer, read the curling iron barrel size guide.

Worked case: replacing a 26 mm clip tong. Your old tong measures 26 mm across the barrel and you liked its size. The Classic Tong is the like-for-like diameter. The Grand Tong is the one to look at only if you want a larger barrel than the one you have.

Worked case: 32 mm or a taper? A listing shows a “32 mm” tool next to a tapered wand. The Grand Tong’s 32 mm is one diameter along a clip barrel. A taper has a wide end and a narrow end, and each is a separate figure. Compare like with like: the Grand Tong against each end of the taper, never against an average of the two.

Buying either tong second-hand? Ask for a photo of the name on the handle as well as the barrel. Classic and Grand share the Chronos Curve styling, so the printed name is a quicker check than judging a 6 mm difference from a photo.

If a seller or a retailer lists a different diameter for either tong, check the ghd UK listing for the exact model before you rely on it.
