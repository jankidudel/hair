Quick answer

Choose Airwrap Origin if you mainly want to dry, brush and curl with the three tools in its box. Choose Airwrap i.d. if connected curling or its wider attachment selection suits your routine. Choose Co-anda2x if the specific Straight+Wavy box includes a straightening or shaping tool you would use often enough to justify a different system.

Match the kit to the work

For drying and loose waves, start with the Origin Fast dryer and long barrel. If you want a connected curling sequence and another shape of barrel, compare the i.d. box. If you would use straightening as a separate weekly job, check that the Co-anda2x listing names Air Straightener 2x. Dyson also uses the name AirSmooth2x for a different attachment in other listings. Choose an attachment for the job you do most often

If your goal is to dry and smooth your hair in one session, compare the drying head and the brush first. Which head would you use on the shortest section, and what would you use all the way through the lengths? The Origin box may cover both if your routine is simple. If you already dry with a separate dryer and want this purchase chiefly for curls, compare the supplied barrel shapes and check whether you would use an app-guided sequence every time or prefer to control the timing yourself.

If you bought an Airwrap before, put your existing body and attachments beside the new kit list. Mark which jobs you already do successfully and which part actually limits you. Give a new attachment credit only if it either replaces a step you already do or adds a step you expect to repeat. If you are counting an old attachment toward the new setup, confirm its compatibility with that exact body before relying on it.
