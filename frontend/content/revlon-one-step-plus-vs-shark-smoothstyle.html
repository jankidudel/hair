Quick answer

Choose the Revlon One-Step Plus for a brush-led blowout with selectable settings and a detachable head. Choose the Shark SmoothStyle HT202 if you also want its separate heated-comb mode for already-dry hair. If you own a tool you like for between-wash touch-ups, compare the two brushes mainly on the blowout you do after washing.

Decide which mode you would repeat

If you blow out your hair after washing and rarely touch it up, first compare how easily both heads can turn under the shortest layer. If you often want to smooth already-dry lengths between washes, look at how you would guide a section over the Shark heated comb. That is the product difference you would use on a second day.

Use the exact US model for the comparison. Shark’s documentation for another market may describe a different box or electrical configuration. For other Revlon sizes and controls, see Revlon Original vs Plus. For a general buying choice, see hot-air brushes.

Choose for your first and second day

Start with the finish you want immediately after washing. If you want the brush to dry while lifting the roots or shaping the ends, compare the clearance of the two oval heads and where your grip puts the selector. With your current tool cool and unplugged, check whether you can get the head beneath a short front layer and still move it out cleanly. Then decide which head seems easier to guide through the lengths, where the movement will be repeated more often.

Next, picture the first time you touch up the blowout on already-dry hair. If you usually leave the lengths alone or use another tool successfully, a second mode may contribute little to the decision. If you often smooth sections between washes and want to use the same handle, look at how the Shark instructions describe the dry mode. Follow the preparation and control directions for whichever mode you use.

Check what fits in your room as well as your routine

If both choices fit the type of blowout you want, look at what happens after you finish. Compare storing the detachable Revlon head and handle with the space you have for a complete assembled tool after it cools. Consider whether you prefer one place for a full-sized handle or separate space for two parts, especially if you put the brush away after each use.

Check the photo and model code on the offer for the US HT202 or the exact Plus listing you intend to buy. If style or colour options change the item in the cart, read the product name again before relying on the heat settings, included pieces or directions described here.
